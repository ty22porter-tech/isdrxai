iSDRX AI / CUSTOMER EDUCATION

How to Use iSDRX AI

HeyGen production package

Audience: new sales users. Format: 16:9, 1920 × 1080. Target runtime: approximately 6–7 minutes, including short pauses for on-screen actions. Language: US English.

This package provides the script, scene prompts, and recording instructions for a HeyGen video. It is not a rendered video or an uploaded HeyGen project. The interface was checked against the current app on September 19, 2026. Account permissions and enabled features may affect what users see.

Copy-ready master prompt

Create a clear, practical customer onboarding video titled “How to Use iSDRX AI.” Use the 12 scenes and narration below in order. Do not summarize away the setup, save-versus-send warnings, or final checklist. Use a friendly, professional presenter with a calm US English voice at approximately 130–140 words per minute. Suggested pronunciation: “eye ess dee ar ex, A I”; verify the brand pronunciation before export.

Use a white background, navy-blue headings and accents, and clean sans-serif typography. Show the presenter full-size only in the introduction and closing. During demonstrations, prioritize readable screen recordings or actual screenshots, with a small presenter inset positioned away from controls. Use restrained cursor highlights and slow zooms. Add accurate captions. Keep music very quiet or omit it.

Use only supplied actual app images and recordings. Do not invent app screens, buttons, customer records, testimonials, bookings, or performance statistics. If footage is missing, use an honest numbered instruction card with the exact labels from this script—not a fabricated app interface. Use fictional sample data and keep “TRAINING EXAMPLE — SAMPLE DATA” visible throughout demonstrations. Never display passwords, keys, real prospect details, or private account information.

Treat the NARRATION blocks as spoken copy. Treat SCENE PROMPT, SCREEN ACTIONS, and ON-SCREEN TEXT as production directions only. Do not read those directions aloud. Do not show a live send action completing: use a safe demonstration account, stop before confirmation, and explain what the button does.

Scene 1 — What you will learn

Suggested duration: 20 seconds.

NARRATION

Welcome to iSDRX AI. In this walkthrough, you’ll set up your business, find prospects, create a campaign, and choose how to send your outreach. Then we’ll review results and explore gift suggestions. Start with a small, carefully reviewed group of prospects before expanding your outreach.

SCENE PROMPT

Show a professional presenter beside the title “How to Use iSDRX AI.” Reveal five numbered labels: Set up, Find prospects, Create a campaign, Send outreach, Review results. Use a navy-and-white layout and an understated transition into the app.

SCREEN ACTIONS

Show the signed-in Overview page if suitable training footage is available. Do not include admin-only navigation in this customer walkthrough.

ON-SCREEN TEXT

Your first outreach workflow

Scene 2 — Set up your business

Suggested duration: 35 seconds.

NARRATION

If you’re asked what kind of business you run, choose the option that matches your services. Next, open Settings. Confirm your seller industry and enter your Seller Company Name, your name, and phone number. Add your Business Mailing Address and Calendly Booking Link. Review your sending and reply-to details, then click Save Settings. If your platform sending address hasn’t been provisioned, ask your administrator to complete that setup before sending.

SCENE PROMPT

Use actual Settings footage. Slowly highlight the company details, mailing address, booking link, and Save Settings button as they are mentioned. Show fictional business information. Never show credentials or suggest connecting Gmail with an app password.

SCREEN ACTIONS

Settings → complete business fields → check email setup → Save Settings. If showing the first-login industry selector, capture it separately; do not imply it appears on every login.

ON-SCREEN TEXT

Complete Settings before outreach
Save Settings does not send a campaign

Scene 3 — Find relevant prospects

Suggested duration: 30 seconds.

NARRATION

Open Prospect Search. Enter the city and state you want to target, then choose the prospect industry. Depending on your business type, you may see additional filters. Click Generate Leads. Review the companies and available contact details before choosing your prospects. Research can return missing or unverified information, so don’t assume that every name or email address is confirmed.

SCENE PROMPT

Show the actual Prospect Search page. Highlight City, State, Industry, and Generate Leads in sequence. Show a short, clearly labeled transition while results load. Do not imply instant results or guaranteed verified contact details.

SCREEN ACTIONS

Prospect Search → set targeting fields → Generate Leads → review results. Use sample responses or approved training records; no real lead research is required to make this video.

ON-SCREEN TEXT

Choose your market → Generate Leads → Review

Scene 4 — Save or import prospects

Suggested duration: 25 seconds.

NARRATION

Click Add on a result you want to keep, or use Add All after reviewing the full list. Adding a prospect saves it to your pipeline; it does not send an email. On the Prospects page, you can also choose Add Prospect to enter a contact manually, or Import CSV to bring in a list. Review the import results and resolve any failed rows.

SCENE PROMPT

Show Add on a search result, then transition to the Prospects page. Highlight Add Prospect and Import CSV. Use a prominent but simple “Saved ≠ Sent” callout.

SCREEN ACTIONS

Search result → Add → Prospects. For a separate import demonstration, use a tiny fictional CSV, preview it, and show success and error handling accurately. Do not import real customer data.

ON-SCREEN TEXT

Saved does not mean sent

Scene 5 — Create and review your campaign

Suggested duration: 40 seconds.

NARRATION

Open Campaigns and choose Create Campaign. Give the campaign a clear name, select its targeting details, and describe your offer. Use Generate with AI to help prepare the message, then review and edit the copy. Check your company details, claims, tone, and next step. Review the follow-up messages and timing too. Choose whether interested prospects should receive a meeting request or a questionnaire first. Save the campaign and activate it when it’s ready. Creating or activating a campaign does not, by itself, send it to prospects.

SCENE PROMPT

Show the actual campaign editor. Highlight the name, offer description, AI generation control, editable message, follow-up steps, and response options. Zoom into a short fictional draft, not a wall of unreadable text. End with a “Review before sending” card.

SCREEN ACTIONS

Campaigns → Create Campaign → fill fields → Generate with AI → review and edit → preview → save. Show Activate only for an approved training campaign, without enrolling recipients.

ON-SCREEN TEXT

AI drafts. You review.
Check the opening message AND follow-ups

Scene 6 — Choose a sending method

Suggested duration: 25 seconds.

NARRATION

You have two main ways to send outreach. For a manual send, open Prospects, select the intended recipients, and choose Send Campaign Email. Select an active campaign and check everything before clicking Send. That final button sends email. If you want automated follow-ups instead, use Autopilot. Avoid enrolling people who are already receiving the same outreach through another workflow.

SCENE PROMPT

Use a two-column instruction card: “Manual: Send Campaign Email” and “Automated: Autopilot.” Show actual manual-send dialog footage if available. Stop with the Send button visible, not clicked.

SCREEN ACTIONS

Prospects → select sample recipients → Send Campaign Email → select active campaign. Stop before Send.

ON-SCREEN TEXT

Send is a live email action

Scene 7 — Start Autopilot carefully

Suggested duration: 35 seconds.

NARRATION

Open Autopilot and click Add Prospects. If you see Quick setup needed, use Go to Settings and complete the missing details first. Choose your campaign and select the prospects you want to enroll. Without a campaign, the app offers a standard three-step AI sequence. Check the recipient count and sequence before continuing. Start Autopilot can begin sending immediately, so click it only when you’re ready. You can pause future automation, but pausing cannot recall emails already sent.

SCENE PROMPT

Show actual Autopilot footage and the Add Prospects dialog. Highlight the campaign selector and selected-recipient count. Before the start control, display “Can send immediately.” Show the location of Pause on an existing fictional active row without inventing a successful send.

SCREEN ACTIONS

Autopilot → Add Prospects → choose campaign → select prospects → review Start Autopilot count. Do not click Start Autopilot against real recipients while recording.

ON-SCREEN TEXT

Start Autopilot can send immediately
Pause stops future automation—not past emails

Scene 8 — Review activity and replies

Suggested duration: 30 seconds.

NARRATION

Return to Overview and Autopilot to review your activity. Open Emails to see the Email Log, including sent messages and available engagement indicators. Use those indicators as context, not proof that someone wants to buy. Review replies before deciding your next step. In Autopilot, Log Reply lets you enter a reply received elsewhere, but Log and Auto-Respond may send a response. It is not just a note-taking button.

SCENE PROMPT

Show sample activity counters, then the actual Email Log with fictional rows. Highlight Sent At, Status, and Engagement. If showing the reply dialog, emphasize that its confirmation can respond automatically.

SCREEN ACTIONS

Overview → Autopilot → Emails. Optional: open Log Reply on a training record and stop before Log & Auto-Respond.

ON-SCREEN TEXT

Engagement is a signal—not a guaranteed lead
Log & Auto-Respond may send a message

Scene 9 — Manage meeting follow-up

Suggested duration: 25 seconds.

NARRATION

Keep your Calendly Booking Link current in Settings. Depending on the configured response rules, interested prospects may receive a meeting invitation automatically. Review Appointments and confirm booking details in Calendly when needed. Sending a booking link, clicking a link, and confirming a meeting are different events. If bookings aren’t appearing as expected, ask your administrator to check your account’s Calendly synchronization setup.

SCENE PROMPT

Show the Calendly Booking Link field and then the actual Appointments page or an honest instruction card. Use three distinct labels: Invitation sent, Link clicked, Meeting confirmed. Do not animate a click turning automatically into a confirmed booking.

SCREEN ACTIONS

Settings → review booking link → Appointments. Do not imply that every external Calendly booking is automatically synchronized for every account.

ON-SCREEN TEXT

A booking-link click is not a confirmed meeting

Scene 10 — Optional: research a thoughtful gift

Suggested duration: 35 seconds.

NARRATION

For a more personal approach, open Gift Suggestions on a prospect search result. Set a budget from ten to one hundred dollars and generate ideas. Read the research summary before choosing anything. If no personal facts are verified, the app may suggest general or name-personalized gifts instead. Retailer links are searches, not guaranteed listings. For a saved prospect, you can compare prices, but check shipping, taxes, and the final checkout total yourself. Gift Suggestions does not purchase or send a gift.

SCENE PROMPT

Use the supplied real-interface gift screenshots. Show the $25 budget, then the no-verified-personal-facts research summary, then the comparison with unknown shipping. Retain the training-data banner. Do not claim the sample retailer is a real offer or infer hobbies from the fictional prospect’s name.

SCREEN ACTIONS

Use gift screenshots 02, 03, and 05 in that order. Explain that unsaved previews are discarded when closed. Verify the recipient’s employer gift policy before any purchase. Gift Suggestions is advisory; optional Gift Outreach is a separate workflow and is not demonstrated here.

ON-SCREEN TEXT

Research first. Verify the total. Check gift policy.
No automatic purchase

Scene 11 — Optional: calling and your daily routine

Suggested duration: 25 seconds.

NARRATION

If your administrator has enabled the caller pilot, use Caller Workspace to review assigned call tasks and record outcomes. If the pilot isn’t enabled, you can skip this step. For your daily routine, review replies and appointments, check active outreach, correct missing prospect details, and pause anything that needs attention. Keep the next action clear for each prospect.

SCENE PROMPT

Use a clean daily-checklist card with an optional Caller Workspace inset only when genuine pilot-enabled footage is available. Do not depict a built-in phone dialer, automatic call placement, or synchronized recordings unless those functions are separately verified and demonstrated.

SCREEN ACTIONS

Optional Caller Workspace → queue/outcome controls. Show feature availability honestly; do not expose administrator tools in the customer guide.

ON-SCREEN TEXT

Daily: Replies → Meetings → Outreach → Next actions
Calling features depend on account access

Scene 12 — Your first-session checklist

Suggested duration: 25 seconds.

NARRATION

You’re ready to begin. Complete Settings, save a small group of relevant prospects, review a campaign, and choose manual sending or Autopilot. Check your recipients and message one more time before sending. Then return regularly to review replies and meetings. Remember: saving prepares your work. Send and Start Autopilot take action. Start small, review carefully, and build from there.

SCENE PROMPT

Return to the presenter beside a five-item checklist. Finish on a navy-and-white title card: “Start with Settings.” Hold the final checklist for three seconds. Do not add promises of guaranteed meetings or sales.

SCREEN ACTIONS

No live send. End on the checklist or Settings navigation.

ON-SCREEN TEXT

1. Complete Settings
2. Save prospects
3. Review your campaign
4. Choose how to send
5. Review results

Assemble in HeyGen

1. Start a landscape video and select a presenter and voice appropriate for customer training.
2. Paste the master prompt into the prompt-based creation workflow if available. Otherwise create 12 scenes manually using this package.
3. Add each scene’s NARRATION as the spoken script. Use the corresponding SCENE PROMPT as visual direction.
4. Upload your actual recordings and the supplied gift screenshots. For missing footage, use instruction cards—not invented app screens.
5. Add captions and keep callouts clear of controls. Review brand pronunciation and the exact labels Send, Start Autopilot, and Log & Auto-Respond.
6. Preview the entire video. Adjust scene lengths to the spoken audio; the suggested timings are estimates, not a timed subtitle file.
7. Check readability at normal playback size, sample-data labeling, account-specific availability, and all save-versus-send warnings.
8. Export in 1080p if available in your HeyGen plan. This package does not perform the HeyGen upload or render.

Pre-export accuracy checklist

- No real customer information, passwords, or credentials are visible.
- No live emails, purchases, calls, or enrollment actions occur during capture.
- Settings and prospect saves are not described as sending.
- Start Autopilot and Log & Auto-Respond are clearly described as actions that can send.
- Contact research and engagement are not represented as certainty.
- Booking-link clicks are not presented as confirmed appointments.
- Gift research is limited to available public evidence; no private-profile access is promised.
- Optional caller and Calendly behavior is not promised for every account.
- Any changed UI labels are updated before recording or publishing this training video.
