---
name: Research source boundaries
description: Rules for accurately describing prospect research, LinkedIn data, and first-party engagement tracking.
---

Describe research coverage by the source actually observed. Official company pages and linked careers/announcement pages are not equivalent to broad public-web, news, funding, LinkedIn, or social monitoring.

**Why:** Overbroad source language can imply the platform verified events it had no collector capable of observing. A LinkedIn profile URL does not prove posts or activity, and a tracked CTA click does not prove a general website visit.

**How to apply:** Require sourced text, URL, date, and company/person matching before treating public activity as evidence. Keep LinkedIn/social activity absent unless a supported provider supplies it. Distinguish tracked outreach events and anonymous CTA clicks from named-prospect website behavior.
