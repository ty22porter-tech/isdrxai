---
name: Company demographic research
description: Evidence rules for filling missing prospect demographics with AI-assisted public web research.
---

Use Apollo as the first source for company demographics. When Apollo does not provide employee count—even when it finds no contact—AI-assisted web research may supply the estimate only with medium/high confidence, an explanation, and inspectable public source URLs.

**Why:** Company names collide, web estimates conflict, and an uncited model answer would be unsafe to present as prospect intelligence.

**How to apply:** Match the official domain and location where available; prefer first-party pages, government records, regulatory filings, and reputable company-data sources. Reject low-confidence, uncited, invalid, or materially conflicting estimates. Never infer size from industry averages or search popularity. Cache successful and failed research to control latency and credit use.
