---
name: Gift outreach campaign decisions
description: Why gift mode has eight steps, approval defaults, and delivery evidence boundaries.
---

- Gift mode means eight steps, not nine; standard mode remains seven. The gift action is step 2 and the package follow-up replaces the standard generic follow-up as step 3. Preserve the original standard content rather than deleting it.
  **Why:** The original attachment simultaneously asked to add two steps and retain seven while calling the result eight. Although the submitted radio selected nine, the user's accompanying written clarification explicitly required eight when enabled and seven when disabled.
  **How to apply:** Prefer the written clarification when changing sequence labels or progression. Never reinterpret completed steps when toggling mode; pause or explicitly convert only untouched enrollments.

- Gift Outreach must start disabled with Review Before Sending as its default mode. Automatic mode requires an intentional user selection.
  **Why:** The feature can send real messages about physical gifts, with approval, timing, and gift-policy consequences.
  **How to apply:** Bind approvals to the reviewed content and invalidate them after relevant changes. A missing video must not silently replace an approved video message with an unreviewed email.

- Carrier delivery confirmation and a user-entered confirmation are different evidence sources. Video opens, thumbnail clicks, playback, and appointments are also distinct facts.
  **Why:** The user requires accurate delivery language and separate engagement measurements. No carrier tracking provider was selected during initial implementation.
  **How to apply:** Label manual delivery confirmation honestly until a carrier integration exists. Never infer receipt from an expected date, viewing from an email open, or a booking from a link click.

- Gift recommendations are a pre-send decision aid, distinct from gift follow-up drafts. Choosing an idea never authorizes a purchase, marks delivery, or approves outreach.
  **Why:** The user explicitly wanted help choosing gifts, not directions to an existing post-send message editor. No purchasing or live retailer-price integration was authorized.
  **How to apply:** Keep ideas available before gift-record creation and use only known business context. Label generated prices as estimates, not current quotes.
