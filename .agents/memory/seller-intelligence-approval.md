---
name: Seller intelligence approval
description: Approval and source-precedence rules for seller facts used in matching and outreach.
---

Treat Company Intelligence marked Needs Review as discovery-only. Seller-specific claims may enter matching and automatic outreach only after the profile is Ready.

**Why:** Incomplete website extraction can contain uncertain seller claims. A visible review warning is not enough protection if automated outreach still treats those claims as approved.

**How to apply:** Keep user edits as per-field overrides rather than freezing the full displayed profile. Rebuild untouched factual fields from approved custom inputs and current website analysis. Service priorities are preferences, not seller facts: use them only in seller-match scoring, never as approved capabilities or outreach claims.
