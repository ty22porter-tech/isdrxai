---
name: Seller intelligence approval
description: Approval and source-precedence rules for seller facts used in matching and outreach.
---

Treat uncertain Company Intelligence marked Needs Review as discovery-only. Individually source-backed offerings from successful company-name web research may supply recommendations even if broader narrative completeness still needs review; unsupported fields must not gain approval through that exception.

**Why:** Incomplete public research can contain uncertain seller claims. A visible review warning is not enough protection if automated outreach still treats those claims as approved.

**Why the offering exception:** A live website-free research flow found verified seller services but produced zero recommendations because an unrelated narrative-completeness gate blocked the entire catalog. The user requested recommendations from researched offerings, not an extra mandatory manual approval step.

**How to apply:** Keep user edits as per-field overrides rather than freezing the full displayed profile. Rebuild untouched factual fields from approved custom inputs and current company-name web research. For IT Services and Fleet & Vehicle Wraps, the user-designated priority-services field is an explicitly approved per-client solution catalog and may supply Recommended Outreach claims when a prospect-specific challenge semantically matches. In other profiles, priorities remain preferences rather than seller facts.

Use the Settings company name and “Your Industry” to research seller offerings through web search; do not use the submitted website as the learning foundation or a prerequisite.

**Why:** The user explicitly chose the research approach demonstrated with Truss Ugavi and Forte: independently discover the seller's actual offerings and rebrands, distinguish the prospect's business from industries it serves, and separate supported facts from possible concerns. “Your Industry” describes the seller, not the prospect.

**How to apply:** Official websites discovered through search remain valid evidence. The optional Settings website is contact/business information, not a seller-research trigger. Do not let a website-only edit invalidate confirmed seller facts.

Recruiting & Staffing is an explicit exception to the company-name-only website rule: use the supplied recruiter website as official identity and offerings context.

**Why:** The user's recruiting workflow explicitly starts with entering the seller website and analyzing the seller's actual recruiting services.

**How to apply:** Website changes may trigger recruiter analysis; preserve the established company-name behavior for the other verticals. Only carry user-approved overrides within the same seller company and selling industry.
