---
name: AI+ billing model
description: The approved sales-assisted pricing and activation policy for AI+ services.
---

AI+ uses sales-assisted billing rather than public self-service checkout. Charge the setup fee once, then activate one agreed fixed monthly service. Additional Caller remains quote-only while its scope and price vary.

**Why:** Human-service capacity, onboarding readiness, and account fit must be confirmed before recurring service begins. A single variable automatic price would also misrepresent the quoted Additional Caller range.

**How to apply:** Require an active base subscription, customer agreement, and staff approval before activating AI+ billing. Keep public calls to action sales-led until capacity and service levels are standardized.
