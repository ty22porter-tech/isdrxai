# iSDRX AI User Guide

**Your guide to finding prospects, reviewing opportunities, preparing outreach, and managing follow-up.**

Use this guide alongside the left navigation menu. Available features depend on your account, selected selling industry, and administrator configuration. Button wording may vary slightly between sections.

## Start here: your first successful workflow

1. **Settings:** Confirm your company details, selling industry, approved services, sender information, and booking link.
2. **Prospect Search:** Find companies in your target area and review their research.
3. **Prospects:** Save qualified contacts and check their information.
4. **Campaigns:** Prepare an email sequence and review the wording.
5. **Choose your outreach workflow:** Use reviewed individual emails, manual follow-up in Sequences, or intentional enrollment in Autopilot.
6. **Monitor results:** Check Emails, Calls, Appointments, and Overview. Review longer-term results in Leadership.

**Before sending:** Confirm the recipient, relevance of your offer, sender identity, signature, and booking details. Generating a draft is different from sending it. Autopilot enrollment can start sending automatically.

## Settings

**Purpose:** Give the app accurate information about your business before it researches prospects or prepares messages.

### Set up your account

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Select the correct selling industry: Janitorial, IT Services, Fleet & Vehicle Wraps, or Recruiting & Staffing.
3. Enter or confirm your business name, website, sender name, reply email, and business address.
4. Review the company intelligence and approved services or capabilities. Correct missing or inaccurate information.
5. Review your signature and any supported collateral or media you intend to use.
6. Add your Calendly booking link if you want prospects to book meetings.
7. Save your changes.

**Business address:** Keep this accurate because it is used in the outreach footer.

**Company intelligence:** Describes your company and what you offer. It is separate from research about a prospect. Do not approve services, certifications, results, or guarantees your company cannot support.

### Check email readiness

Look for **Platform email ready** and review the **Deliverability** checks. Select **Recheck** after a sending-configuration issue has been addressed. If platform email is unavailable, contact your administrator.

The platform handles sending; follow the configuration shown in the app rather than entering an email password elsewhere.

### Connect Calendly

A saved booking URL makes a link available for outreach. A connected Calendly account supports the available synchronization features. Use **Connect**, **Sync now**, or **Disconnect** as appropriate. Saving a URL alone does not mean calendar synchronization is connected.

### Optional automation settings

Review AI employee instructions, engagement automation, and gift settings when available. Enable only the workflows you intend to use. These settings can affect subsequent outreach and follow-up.

**Completion check:** Your company identity, approved offerings, sending status, signature, and booking information are correct.

## Prospect Search

**Purpose:** Discover companies that may fit your offer and evaluate the evidence before adding contacts to your pipeline.

### Run a search

1. Open **Prospect Search**.
2. Enter your target city and state.
3. Select the prospect industries you serve.
4. Enter the requested number of results and any optional filters.
5. Select **Generate**.
6. Review the completed results before adding prospects.

**Allow two minutes or longer after selecting Generate.** Processing may include employer discovery, company research, hiring verification, service-fit checks, and contact lookup. The requested result count is a target, not a guarantee of qualified companies or usable contacts.

For Recruiting searches, use **Reconnect saved search** if the connection is interrupted. This reconnects to the existing search instead of repeating discovery. Use the available failed-research retry action to recover failed research rather than repeatedly generating new searches.

### How Recruiting prospects are identified

Recruiting & Staffing searches identify **hiring employers, not job candidates**.

The app uses your location, selected industries, and filters to discover published hiring information from employer careers pages, applicant tracking systems, and accessible job boards. Research then checks the hiring employer's identity, job titles and locations, posting status, and alignment with your approved recruiting services.

Duplicate listings are consolidated where possible. One listing does not necessarily mean several hires. An unknown posting date remains unknown and does not establish urgency.

Employer industry and job function are considered separately. For example, a software engineer opening at a manufacturer does not automatically make the employer a technology company.

Agency restrictions and qualification checks can prevent automatic contact lookup. For eligible employers, Apollo may return relevant department leaders or business contacts. The contact's responsibility for the hiring site and authority to hire may remain unconfirmed.

A published vacancy indicates a potential conversation. It does not prove hiring difficulty, budget, urgency, or willingness to use an agency.

### Understand Recruiting opportunity scores

| Score | Label | How to interpret it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–29 | Low Priority | Limited evidence or insufficient supported alignment. Review before pursuing. |
| 30–59 | Possible Fit | Enough supporting information to consider the employer, with further qualification needed. |
| 60–79 | Strong Fit | Stronger hiring evidence and service alignment. Prioritize a discovery review. |
| 80–100 | High Priority | The strongest combination of supporting signals under the scoring model. |

Scores consider employer industry fit, verified hiring evidence, supported service matches, relevant stakeholders, evidence freshness, and explicit urgency or growth hiring. Missing evidence can reduce a score even when a real opportunity exists.

**The score is not a purchase probability or an engagement score.** A score of 76 does not mean a 76% chance of winning business. A research failure also does not establish that the employer is a poor fit.

Select **View Score Breakdown** for the points and evidence behind a particular result. Other selling industries use their own opportunity assessments; do not assume Recruiting thresholds describe every other scoring model.

### Search-card actions and labels

| Item | Meaning or action |
| --- | --- |
| **View Score Breakdown** | Opens the scoring categories, supporting evidence, and research details. |
| **Refresh research** | Rechecks opportunity research; available within the research details. |
| **Gift Suggestions** | Opens prospect gift ideas. Opening suggestions does not send a gift. |
| **Add** | Saves the prospect to your pipeline. This action alone does not send outreach. |
| **Added** | Confirms the card is already associated with a saved pipeline prospect. |
| **Retry** after an Add error | Attempts to save the prospect again. |
| **Retry contact lookup** | Attempts enrichment again when contact details remain unresolved and qualification permits it. |
| **Real contact found** | A contact was returned; hiring authority and reachability are not guaranteed. |
| **Likely department contact** | The contact's title appears relevant; site responsibility and hiring authority may be unknown. |
| **Hiring fit verified** | The Recruiting result passed the app's contact-eligibility checks. It is a status badge, not a call button. |
| **Needs filter review** | Qualification or filter requirements remain unresolved. |
| **Direct line unverified** | The number is not confirmed as the person's direct line. It may be a company number. |
| **Postings awaiting verification** | Listings were found, but active status or other required evidence remains incomplete. |

If contact lookup is disabled, read the blocker displayed on the card. Refresh research when appropriate; a higher opportunity score does not override qualification restrictions.

**Completion check:** You have reviewed employer identity, relevant evidence, service fit, contact limitations, and any restrictions before saving the prospect.

## Fit scores: what they mean and how to use them

**Fit answers “How well does this opportunity align with our business and the evidence?” It does not answer “Will this person buy?”**

The opportunity score uses these labels:

| Score | Label | Recommended interpretation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–29 | Low Priority | Review missing evidence or weak alignment before spending more time. |
| 30–59 | Possible Fit | A potential match worth qualifying; important facts may still be missing. |
| 60–79 | Strong Fit | Stronger supporting evidence and alignment; prioritize a relevant discovery conversation. |
| 80–100 | High Priority | The strongest combination of evidence under the model; confirm the remaining buying and contact questions. |

The underlying scoring factors depend on your selling industry. Use **View Score Breakdown** to understand the points for that specific prospect.

### Possible Fit does not mean a bad prospect

A Recruiting prospect might have a confirmed employer, a matching service recommendation, and one verified opening, but no recent published posting date or confirmed relevant contact. That can produce a Possible Fit result. Missing information can lower the score even when the opportunity is worth a conversation.

**Example:** A score of 51 means the prospect is in the Possible Fit range. Review the opening and service recommendation, confirm the contact's responsibility, and ask whether outside staffing support would be useful. It does not mean a 51% chance of a sale.

### Strong Fit is a priority signal, not a guarantee

**Example:** A score of 76 is Strong Fit. It supports prioritizing research review or a conversation, but does not establish agency interest, budget, purchasing authority, or a direct phone number. Even a High Priority prospect still requires qualification.

### Distinguish the indicators

| Indicator | What it tells you | What it does not establish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Opportunity Score** | Overall evidence-based opportunity alignment. | Purchase probability or recent engagement. |
| **Commercial fit** in Recruiting | Alignment from confirmed industry fit, service match, and a relevant stakeholder title. | Verified urgency or a percentage chance of winning. |
| **Evidence confidence** | Strength of the hiring evidence under the model, including available active listings and freshness. | That every field or inference is independently confirmed. |
| **Real contact found** | A contact was returned. | Verified hiring authority, interest, or guaranteed reachability. |
| **Hiring fit verified** | Recruiting qualification checks passed for contact eligibility. | Willingness to use an agency or permission to assume a hiring problem. |
| **Fit to Call** | An opportunity indicator used in other selling industries. | An automatic call, confirmed phone ownership, or a scheduled callback. |
| **Hot/Warm or engagement indicators** | Recorded interactions or engagement classification. | A purchase commitment or the same thing as opportunity fit. |

Commercial fit and Opportunity Score can differ. For example, an employer may strongly match your services but have limited dated hiring evidence. Neither number should be read as a probability.

A **Research unavailable** result is a failed or incomplete research attempt, not a verified finding that the employer has no need. A qualification blocker can still prevent contact lookup even when a score is displayed.

### Use scores in this order

1. Read research warnings and qualification blockers.
2. Open the score breakdown and supporting sources.
3. Confirm the offer fits the identified business need or role.
4. Review the contact's department, location, and phone limitations.
5. Choose an appropriate next action: more research, a discovery email, or a call.

Do not spend time trying to increase a number without improving the underlying evidence or targeting.

## Prospects

**Purpose:** Manage the companies and contacts saved in your pipeline.

1. Open **Prospects** and use the available search, filters, and sorting controls to find a record.
2. Select a prospect to open its details.
3. Check the contact, company, research, notes, and current status.
4. Use the available editing or enrichment controls to address incomplete information.
5. Review activity before sending another message or enrolling the prospect in outreach.

### Import a list

1. Select **Import CSV**.
2. Upload your CSV and review the field mapping and duplicate-handling option.
3. Check the preview before confirming the import.
4. Review added, updated, skipped, and failed rows.
5. Correct the reported rows and use the available retry or download options.

Importing a contact does not verify its opportunity evidence or make every record ready for outreach.

### Work from a prospect's detail page

- **Generate AI Email / Regenerate:** Creates or replaces a draft for review.
- **Send Email:** Sends the reviewed message when account and prospect requirements permit it.
- **Generate call script:** Prepares talking points; review them before a call.
- **Log Call Outcome:** Records the actual result and notes.
- **Appointment controls:** Record or manage a meeting using the options provided.
- **Activity, Emails, Calls, and Gift Outreach tabs:** Show the record's related history.

**Completion check:** The saved record is accurate, and your next action fits its existing activity and status.

## Campaigns

**Purpose:** Build reusable outreach templates and follow-up timing for a target audience.

### Create a campaign

1. Open **Campaigns** and use the campaign creation control.
2. Enter a descriptive name, such as “Dallas Manufacturing — Temp-to-Hire.”
3. Select the campaign type and target industry.
4. Add a description to explain the audience and offer.
5. Generate or write the introductory email and follow-up steps.
6. Review subjects, wording, and day delays.
7. Save the campaign and use the available preview before activation or enrollment.

Keep employer-specific facts out of a reusable industry template. Personalization fills in the details for each prospect.

### Personalization fields

| Field | Replaced with |
| --- | --- |
| `{{contactName}}` | The contact's first name. |
| `{{companyName}}` | The prospect's company name. |
| `{{city}}` | The prospect's city. |
| `{{state}}` | The prospect's state. |
| `{{industry}}` | The prospect industry supplied during template merging. |
| `{{jobOpenings}}` | A Recruiting paragraph with up to three usable researched job titles and locations, or neutral hiring-priority wording if none are verified. |

For Recruiting emails, put `{{jobOpenings}}` on its own line between paragraphs. Newly generated Recruiting templates include this field. Existing templates can be edited to add it.

Example template:

```text
Hi {{contactName}},

{{jobOpenings}}

If outside staffing support would be useful, we can discuss whether our
approved services fit the roles and timing your team is managing.

Would a short conversation about your hiring priorities be useful?
```

The job-opening field excludes closed, unknown-status, and agency-restricted jobs, and requires usable research. It describes published listings found during research; it does not promise those roles remain open today.

### Review before use

Check the actual personalized email, not only the generic template. Confirm that the role, location, proposed service, company, greeting, and call to action make sense together. Remove unsupported claims about candidates, credentials, results, guarantees, or the employer's needs.

Use **Pause** or **Resume** where offered to manage campaign status. Check Autopilot enrollments separately when you need to control a specific prospect's automation. An active campaign label is not proof that a particular email was sent; verify sending in the activity history.

**Completion check:** All steps are reviewed, timing is intentional, and personalized drafts contain no unresolved placeholders.

## Autopilot

**Purpose:** Run automated outreach for deliberately selected prospects.

**Enrollment can initiate sending.** Finish configuration and review campaign content before confirming enrollment.

1. Open **Autopilot** and address any configuration warning.
2. Select **Add Prospects**.
3. Choose a campaign, or intentionally choose the standard AI sequence without a campaign.
4. Review the selected prospects individually. Use **Select all** only when every displayed prospect is appropriate.
5. Confirm enrollment when you are ready for automated outreach.
6. Monitor each enrollment and use its available pause, resume, or removal controls as needed.

Without a campaign, the standard AI workflow uses an introductory message and subsequent follow-ups. Campaign-based workflows use the configured sequence. Gift-enabled workflows add gift-related steps and holds. Actual processing depends on configuration, eligibility, and sending availability.

Where available, use **Log Reply** to record a reply received outside automatic capture. Log only a real response. Review booking behavior and reply handling in your account's configuration.

Do not manually send the same follow-up while Autopilot is managing it.

**Completion check:** Only intended prospects are enrolled, the selected campaign is correct, and you know where to pause the workflow.

## Gift Outreach

**Purpose:** Coordinate a gift-related follow-up with a campaign and review engagement from personalized video outreach.

### How the gift sections connect

There is not a separate disconnected campaign system. The gift workflow uses a prospect and an existing campaign, with a gift record connecting them.

| Section | Its role in the gift workflow |
| --- | --- |
| **Settings → Gift Outreach** | Enables the feature and sets method, approval mode, timing, timezone, and content defaults. |
| **Campaigns** | Stores the campaign and ordinary email sequence. |
| **Prospect Search → Gift Suggestions** | Researches possible gifts; opening ideas does not start a campaign or send anything. |
| **Prospects → selected prospect → Gift Outreach tab** | Selects the campaign and manages the prospect's gift record, delivery details, draft, approval, and follow-up action. |
| **Autopilot** | Manages enrolled gift sequences and advances them after gift follow-up is sent or explicitly skipped. |
| **Sequences** | Shows eligible follow-up work and holds; directs you to the record or Autopilot when required. |
| **Gift Outreach in the navigation menu** | Primarily displays sent personalized-video outreach and related engagement. It is not an inventory of every gift idea or email-only gift record. |
| **Emails** | Shows the recorded outbound email activity. |

An empty Gift Outreach dashboard does not necessarily mean there are no gift records. Check the prospect's Gift Outreach tab, especially for email-only follow-ups or unsent video drafts.

### Step 1: Configure gift outreach

1. Open **Settings** and find **Gift Outreach**.
2. Enable the feature.
3. Select the default method: AI-written email, personalized video email, or choose separately for each prospect.
4. Select the automation level.
5. Set the delay after delivery and the sent-date fallback delay.
6. Confirm allowed sending days, business hours, and timezone.
7. Review service descriptions, discussion points, scheduling link, and signature.
8. Save the settings.

| Automation level | Behavior |
| --- | --- |
| **Review Before Sending** | Requires approval of the current prepared record. After approval, the queue can send when timing and other requirements pass. |
| **Manual** | Does not send from the automatic gift queue. Use the record's manual send action after preparation and required approval. |
| **Fully Automated** | Can prepare and send an eligible due follow-up without routine approval, subject to sending controls. Risk flags or compliance notes can still require approval. |

Choose **Review Before Sending** while learning the workflow. Approval is not a promise of an immediate send: timing, sending hours, eligibility, and record readiness still apply.

### Step 2: Prepare the campaign and prospect

1. Create or select an appropriate campaign in **Campaigns**.
2. Review its introduction and follow-up wording.
3. Add the qualified prospect to **Prospects** if it is not already saved.
4. Open the prospect and select its **Gift Outreach** tab.
5. Choose the campaign associated with this outreach.
6. Choose Email or Video Email as appropriate and select **Start Gift Outreach**.

Starting the record does not purchase or ship a physical gift. Selecting a gift idea is a pending choice until you save it to the record.

### Step 3: Record the physical gift accurately

Arrange purchasing and physical shipping through your actual fulfillment process. In the prospect's gift record:

1. Enter or select the gift name.
2. Turn on **Gift has been sent** only after it has actually been dispatched.
3. Enter **Date Sent**.
4. Add expected delivery when available.
5. Enter **Confirmed Delivery (Manual)** only when you have confirmation.
6. Add carrier, tracking reference, amount spent, and the gift note when applicable.
7. Select **Save Changes**.

The tracking reference is a stored detail; it does not itself connect to a carrier. Expected delivery is not confirmed delivery.

### Step 4: Prepare the follow-up

1. Select Email or Video Email on the gift record.
2. Save changes before taking another action.
3. For video email, attach the required video through the record's video controls. If no video is available, the configured fallback either pauses the step or prepares an email fallback.
4. Select **Generate AI Draft**.
5. Review the subject, body, recipient, gift references, and scheduling details.
6. Save any edits and approve the current version when required.
7. Let the configured queue handle eligible approved records, or use **Send Manually** in a manual workflow when the record is ready.

**Send Manually sends the gift follow-up email or video email. It does not order or ship a physical gift.** Editing a record can invalidate its prior approval; review and approve the updated version when required.

### How follow-up timing is calculated

The app uses confirmed delivery first, expected delivery second, and sent date as a fallback. It applies the configured business-day delay and permitted sending window. A scheduled time is a processing target, not a guarantee of delivery at that exact minute.

If only expected delivery is available, do not write as though receipt has been confirmed. If the gift was not sent, leave that status accurate or explicitly skip the gift step.

### Step 5: Connect the gift workflow to Autopilot

1. Finish reviewing settings, campaign content, and recipients before enrollment; enrollment can start outreach.
2. In **Autopilot**, choose the campaign and intended prospects while gift outreach is enabled.
3. Confirm enrollment and monitor its progress.
4. After the introduction, complete the gift record or explicitly skip the gift action.
5. Prepare and approve the package follow-up as required.
6. After the gift follow-up is sent or skipped, the linked enrollment can proceed to the remaining campaign follow-ups.

The eight-step structure is:

| Stage | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Step 1** | Introductory email. |
| **Step 2** | Gift action: confirm the physical gift was sent, or explicitly skip. |
| **Step 3** | Gift/package follow-up email or video email, governed by the gift record. |
| **Steps 4–8** | Remaining campaign follow-ups. |

The package follow-up replaces the ordinary first follow-up for that linked gift sequence; it should not also be sent separately from Sequences.

An existing standard enrollment is not silently converted when you enable gifts. Where allowed, use the explicit opt-in checkbox on the gift record before its first follow-up. An enrollment that has progressed too far or stopped cannot be converted through that option.

Disabling gift outreach pauses existing gift sequences rather than changing their numbering back to a standard sequence. Replies, bookings, opt-outs, stopped statuses, and other eligibility restrictions can stop further gift outreach.

### Step 6: Monitor the results

- Open the prospect's gift record for preparation, approval, delivery entries, holds, or errors.
- Open **Gift Outreach** for available video engagement and prospect follow-up links after video outreach is sent.
- Open **Emails** for recorded outbound messages.
- Open **Autopilot** for the linked enrollment's progress.

A view or click indicates recorded engagement; it does not confirm receipt of the physical gift or a decision to buy.

### Gift button reference

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Generate ideas / Refresh ideas** | Researches gift suggestions without purchasing or sending anything. |
| **Start Gift Outreach** | Creates the prospect-and-campaign gift record, subject to eligibility and any explicit opt-in. |
| **Save Changes** | Saves record edits; the changed version may require fresh approval. |
| **Discard Changes** | Clears unsaved edits. |
| **Generate AI Draft / Re-generate AI Draft** | Prepares follow-up wording for review. |
| **Approve** | Approves the current record revision where approval is required. |
| **Send Manually** | Sends the prepared gift follow-up message if its requirements pass. |
| **Skip Gift** | Explicitly resolves the gift action as skipped; linked follow-up progression can continue. |
| **View Prospect** | Opens the saved prospect and its detailed history. |
| **Schedule Follow-Up** | Opens the follow-up destination provided on the dashboard card; review and complete the next action there. |

**Completion check:** The physical gift status is accurate, the correct campaign is linked, the current draft has the required approval, and one workflow owns the next send.

## Sequences

**Purpose:** Review prospects due for their next eligible manual follow-up.

1. Open **Sequences**.
2. Expand the relevant campaign group.
3. Check the prospect, next step, and time since the previous message.
4. Review the personalized message using the available preview or review flow.
5. Use the send action only when the next message is appropriate.
6. Select **Replied** only if the prospect actually replied.

Standard campaign templates support an intro plus six follow-ups. Gift-enabled sequences add a gift action and associated follow-up handling. Read any **Managed by Autopilot**, gift hold, or paused status before acting.

If a batch send action is available, review its recipients first. Each email is an outreach action, even when sent as part of a group.

**Completion check:** No duplicate follow-up was sent, and replied or held records were handled appropriately.

## Emails

**Purpose:** Review the email activity log.

1. Open **Emails**.
2. Review the time, prospect, subject, and available activity indicators.
3. Use the prospect link or record to examine the associated history.
4. Compare the log with the campaign or Autopilot workflow when investigating an unexpected message.

An open or click is an engagement signal, not a confirmed buying decision. Privacy tools, forwarding, and automated scanning can affect tracking. A missing open indicator does not prove the recipient never read the message.

**Completion check:** You understand which messages are recorded and which prospects need an appropriate next action.

## Calls

**Purpose:** Review the history of recorded prospecting calls and use those records to plan the next conversation.

### When to use Calls

Use **Calls** when you want to:

- Check whether anyone has already contacted a prospect by phone.
- Review a previous outcome or callback note before reaching out again.
- Confirm that a completed call was recorded.
- Open the prospect's record to prepare a follow-up.
- Review calling activity alongside campaign and appointment history.

**Calls is the call-history page.** Use the prospect detail page to prepare a script and log an individual call, or **Caller Workspace** to work a structured calling queue. Do not assume opening Calls starts an automated dialer.

### Choose the correct place to work

| Your task | Where to go |
| --- | --- |
| Read previous calls and notes | **Calls** or the prospect's **Calls** tab. |
| Prepare for one specific prospect | **Prospects → open prospect → AI Call Script**. |
| Log a basic individual call | **AI Call Script → Log Call Outcome → Save Call Log** on the prospect detail page. |
| Work assigned calls, structured outcomes, or callbacks | **Caller Workspace**, when enabled. |
| Review aggregate calling performance | **Leadership**. |
| Track a resulting meeting | **Appointments** and the related prospect record. |

### Review the call log

1. Open **Calls** from the navigation menu.
2. Review **Time**, **Prospect**, **Status / Outcome**, **Duration**, and **Notes**.
3. Select the row or **View Prospect** to open the related record.
4. Read the notes and recent email activity before deciding to call again.

A blank duration or outcome means it was not available in that log entry; it does not prove no conversation occurred. Basic prospect-page logs and structured Caller Workspace records can contain different levels of detail.

### Prepare and log an individual call

1. Open the prospect from **Prospects** or a call-log row.
2. Review the company, contact title, phone source, opportunity evidence, and prior activity.
3. Select **AI Call Script** to generate talking points.
4. Read the script, key points, and objection suggestions. Correct irrelevant or unsupported wording before using it.
5. Make the call using your configured phone workflow. Generating a script does not itself place a call.
6. Enter accurate notes under **Log Call Outcome** and select **Save Call Log**.
7. Check **Calls** or the prospect's Calls tab to confirm the entry was recorded.

The basic prospect-page log records a completed entry with your notes; it does not offer the full structured outcome selection available in Caller Workspace. Write the actual result in the notes, including voicemail or no answer when applicable. Never log a call simply because you generated a script.

### When a call is appropriate

Consider a call when you have relevant business context and a usable number—for example, to introduce a supported service, clarify an identified hiring need, return an actual callback request, or follow up on a previous conversation.

For Recruiting, a useful opening is: “We identified a published listing for [verified job title]. Are you the right person to ask about how your team handles recruiting support for that role?” Do not assume the contact owns the opening or wants agency help.

A **Strong Fit** score can help prioritize your review, but it does not confirm the number is direct or that the contact wants a call. Read contact limitations and do-not-call or stop instructions first. If the number reaches a company switchboard, ask for the appropriate department rather than claiming a personal connection.

### Record useful notes

Capture who answered, their responsibility, what was discussed, whether the information was confirmed or still uncertain, and the agreed next action. Record an actual callback date and timezone when one is requested.

Example: “Reached the main switchboard. Contact was unavailable. No hiring need confirmed. Reception suggested calling the HR department tomorrow afternoon; no appointment booked.”

**Completion check:** The log accurately states what happened, and any callback, follow-up, or meeting has been recorded in the appropriate workflow.

## Appointments

**Purpose:** Track walkthroughs, consultations, and other meetings.

1. Open **Appointments**.
2. Review upcoming and past appointments.
3. Open the related prospect to review the purpose and recent communication.
4. Use the available status or rescheduling controls to update the record.
5. Confirm time, timezone, location or meeting method, and contact details with the prospect.

A manually recorded appointment is not, by itself, proof that an external calendar invitation or notification was delivered. Review the connected booking workflow and communicate changes as needed.

**Completion check:** Both your record and the prospect's understanding reflect the agreed meeting details.

## Caller Workspace

**Purpose:** Work calling tasks through the enabled caller workflow.

If you see **Caller Pilot Not Enabled**, ask your administrator about access. This workspace depends on account configuration.

1. Open **Caller Workspace**.
2. Review **My Queue**.
3. Select **Claim Next Task** when you are ready to work the next available assignment.
4. Read the prospect details, call instructions or script, and prior notes.
5. Complete the calling activity through the available controls.
6. Record the outcome and notes before completing the task.

Use manual enqueue controls only for records that should enter your calling queue. Do not claim another task while one is already in progress.

### Record the structured outcome

Choose the result that actually occurred:

| Outcome | Use when |
| --- | --- |
| **Connected** | You reached a person; describe the conversation in notes. |
| **Voicemail** | You reached voicemail; note whether you left a message. |
| **No answer** | The attempt was unanswered. |
| **Callback requested** | A callback was requested; enter the required callback time. |
| **Information sent** | Information was actually sent; identify it in notes. |
| **Meeting booked** | A meeting was actually agreed; verify the appointment details separately. |
| **Not interested** | The prospect declined the offer. |
| **Do not call** | The person asked not to be called; record the request clearly. |
| **Wrong number** | The number did not reach the intended contact or company. |

Enter notes and duration where available, then select **Complete Call**. Use **Release back to queue** if you cannot work the task, or **Snooze 4h** when that delay is appropriate. These task controls do not themselves confirm a conversation or book a meeting.

**Completion check:** The task's final outcome is recorded and the queue reflects your completed work.

## Overview

**Purpose:** Understand current activity at a glance.

1. Open **Overview** at the start of your day.
2. Review prospect, email, call, and appointment totals.
3. Inspect pipeline distribution and recent activity.
4. Review engagement indicators and open the related records for context.
5. Decide which prospects, replies, follow-ups, or appointments need attention.

**Opportunity fit and engagement are different:** An opportunity score describes research-based fit. Engagement describes observed interactions. Neither alone confirms a purchase decision.

**Completion check:** You have identified today's most relevant follow-up work.

## Leadership

**Purpose:** Review performance trends and campaign results.

1. Open **Leadership**.
2. Choose **Last 7 Days**, **Last 30 Days**, or **Last 90 Days**.
3. Review new prospects, outreach volume, email open rate, meetings set, and connect rate.
4. Compare outreach activity, pipeline distribution, call outcomes, and campaign performance.
5. Investigate the underlying records before deciding why a metric changed.

Compare consistent time periods and consider the number of records behind each percentage. An open rate is not a reply rate, and a meeting set is not a completed sale.

**Completion check:** You can identify which activity needs adjustment and which records support that conclusion.

## Billing

**Purpose:** Manage the subscription options available to your account.

1. Open **Billing**.
2. Review your displayed subscription or trial status.
3. Use the available billing-management control for account changes.
4. Select **View Plans & Pricing** when comparing plans.
5. Enter a redemption code only if one was provided to you.

If an outreach feature reports a subscription requirement or suspension, review Billing or contact your administrator before repeating the action.

**Completion check:** Your account status and selected plan support the features you intend to use.

## Administrator sections

These sections appear only for authorized administrators.

### Admin

Use **Admin** to review client accounts and the available account, subscription, onboarding, or caller-configuration controls. Verify the selected client before changing settings. A change here can affect another user's access or outreach behavior.

### Platform Leadership

Use **Platform Leadership** for platform-level reporting across the accounts available to your administrator role. Choose the reporting period and distinguish platform-wide totals from an individual client's results.

Routine users should use **Leadership** for their own account's performance.

## Common problems and next steps

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Search is still processing | Allow two minutes or longer. Read the progress message instead of immediately starting another search. |
| Recruiting search connection was interrupted | Use **Reconnect saved search**. |
| HTTP 502 or “Your app is starting” | Allow the app to become available, then reconnect. Report persistent failures to support with the time and action. |
| Research was rate-limited | Allow the indicated retry or waiting period. Avoid repeated new searches. |
| Contact lookup is disabled | Read the qualification blocker and refresh research when appropriate. |
| Email or phone is missing | Retry contact lookup if eligible; do not invent contact details. |
| Add failed with a 404 or other error | Check whether the prospect already exists. Capture the error and ask support to inspect the specific failed request. |
| Recruiting email does not mention openings | Confirm the template contains `{{jobOpenings}}` and the prospect has usable saved hiring research. |
| A merge field remains in a personalized draft | Correct or regenerate the draft before sending. Generic templates intentionally show fields before personalization. |
| Gift follow-up is held | Open the gift record or Autopilot enrollment and resolve the displayed requirement. |
| Caller Workspace is unavailable | Ask your administrator whether caller access is enabled. |
| Sending or billing action is blocked | Review Settings, Billing, and the displayed account warning. |

When requesting help, provide the section, action, approximate time, exact error, and whether you were using Preview/development or the published app. Hide passwords, access keys, and unnecessary personal data.

## Daily operating checklist

- Review Overview and upcoming Appointments.
- Check replies and recent prospect activity before sending follow-ups.
- Work eligible manual Sequences or monitor Autopilot without duplicating its sends.
- Complete caller tasks and record accurate outcomes when applicable.
- Review gift statuses and outstanding holds.
- Research and add qualified prospects as needed.
- Review personalized drafts before manual sending and campaign content before automated enrollment.
- Review Leadership periodically to improve targeting and follow-up.
