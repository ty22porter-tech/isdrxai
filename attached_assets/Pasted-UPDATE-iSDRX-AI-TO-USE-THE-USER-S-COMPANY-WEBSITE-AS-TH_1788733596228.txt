UPDATE iSDRX AI TO USE THE USER'S COMPANY WEBSITE AS THE SELLER INTELLIGENCE SOURCE

IMPORTANT ARCHITECTURE CHANGE:

Do not hard-code specific companies such as Whitlock Building Services,
C4 Wraps, or any future customer into an industry profile.

Instead, use the Company Website entered by the user under:

Settings → Company Website

as the primary source for creating a dynamic Seller Intelligence Profile.

The industry selected under:

Settings → Your Industry

should determine the prospecting framework.

The Company Website should determine:

- What the user's company sells
- Services offered
- Capabilities
- Differentiators
- Certifications
- Industries served
- Value propositions
- Business outcomes
- Proof points
- Relevant terminology
- Competitive positioning
- Calls to action
- Relevant portfolio/examples where available

This allows every customer in the same industry to use the same iSDRX AI
industry model without requiring custom code.


====================================================

        ↓

        ↓
PERSONALIZED EMAIL CAMPAIGN


====================================================
INDUSTRY PROFILE VS SELLER PROFILE
====================================================

Keep these two concepts separate.

INDUSTRY PROFILE

The user's selected industry determines:

- Which types of companies to search
- Relevant target industries
- Relevant job titles
- Industry buying signals
- Search filters
- Industry-specific opportunity scoring
- Typical business challenges
- Relevant research categories

Examples:

IT Services:
Cybersecurity, compliance, infrastructure, IT hiring, cloud, technology
change, managed services opportunities.

Janitorial:
Facility type, building operations, expansion, occupancy, property
management, RFPs, construction, facility needs.

Fleet & Commercial Vehicle Wraps:
Fleet-dependent businesses, field technicians, drivers, vehicle growth,
territory expansion, branding, locations, commercial vehicles.

The industry profile SHOULD NOT define the exact seller's capabilities.


SELLER INTELLIGENCE PROFILE

The seller profile should be dynamically created from the website entered
under Settings → Company Website.

The seller profile determines:

- Seller company name
- Services
- Products
- Capabilities
- Differentiators
- Certifications
- Industries served
- Value proposition
- Problems solved
- Business outcomes
- Proof points
- Portfolio examples
- Customer types
- Relevant calls to action
- Seller terminology


====================================================
WEBSITE ANALYSIS
====================================================

When a valid Company Website is saved:

Analyze the publicly accessible website.

Where possible review relevant pages such as:

- Homepage
- About
- Services
- Solutions
- Industries
- Products
- Portfolio
- Gallery
- Case Studies
- Testimonials
- Certifications
- Why Us
- FAQ

Do not rely only on the homepage if other relevant public pages are
available.

Extract and summarize the information into a structured Seller Intelligence
Profile.

====================================================
WEBSITE PROFILE REFRESH
====================================================

Do NOT analyze the seller website during every prospect search.

This is important for speed and API cost.

Instead:

1. Analyze the website when the user saves or changes the Company Website.

2. Create the Seller Intelligence Profile.

3. Save/cached the intelligence profile.

4. Prospect Search should use the stored intelligence profile.

5. Re-analyze only when:
   - Website URL changes
   - User manually selects Refresh Company Intelligence
   - Profile has not previously been analyzed
   - A future scheduled refresh feature is implemented

This prevents unnecessary repeated website research and AI costs.

SETTINGS UI
====================================================

Keep the existing:

field.

Optionally provide:

View Company Intelligence

Do not require the user to manually enter all services and value propositions
if they have provided a usable website.


====================================================
PROSPECT VALUE MATCHING
====================================================

For every prospect, compare:

SELLER INTELLIGENCE PROFILE
        ↓
PROSPECT BUSINESS
        ↓
VERIFIED PROSPECT SIGNALS
        ↓
POTENTIAL BUSINESS CHALLENGE
        ↓
RELEVANT SELLER CAPABILITY
        ↓
BUSINESS VALUE
        ↓
SALES ANGLE


The AI should answer:

1. Why is this prospect relevant to THIS seller?

2. What business challenge may exist?

3. Which seller capability could address it?

4. What business outcome could the seller potentially provide?

5. What is the strongest sales angle?

6. Which seller differentiator is most relevant?

7. What should the email say?


====================================================
WHY TARGET
====================================================

Why Target should no longer be based only on the generic industry profile.

It should combine:

Prospect information
+
Industry fit
+
Seller intelligence
+
Relevant business challenge
+
Seller capability


Example structure:

Why Target:

[Prospect] operates in [business/facility context], creating a potential need
around [business challenge].

[Seller] provides [specific capability from seller website], which aligns
with this environment by helping organizations [relevant business outcome].

This creates a strong potential fit because [specific connection].


====================================================
POTENTIAL NEEDS
====================================================

Potential Needs should NOT simply list generic products or services.

Use:

BUSINESS CHALLENGE
→
SELLER SOLUTION
→
BUSINESS OUTCOME


Example:

Brand visibility →
The seller's fleet graphics capability could turn vehicles already traveling
through the service area into additional local brand exposure.

Facility consistency →
The seller's customized janitorial program could provide structured cleaning
around the facility's operating schedule.

Cybersecurity exposure →
The seller's managed security services could help strengthen endpoint,
network, and data protection.


====================================================
RECOMMENDED OUTREACH
====================================================

Recommended Outreach should explain which part of the SELLER'S actual value
proposition the salesperson should lead with.

Do not generate:

"Offer cleaning services."

"Offer fleet wraps."

"Offer IT services."

Instead:

"Lead with [specific seller capability] because [verified prospect situation]
creates an opportunity around [business outcome]."


====================================================
PERSONALIZED EMAIL GENERATION
====================================================

Every outreach email should use the Seller Intelligence Profile.

The AI should generate the email from:

PROSPECT BUSINESS
+
VERIFIED SIGNAL
+
BUSINESS CHALLENGE
+
SELLER CAPABILITY
+
SELLER DIFFERENTIATOR
+
BUSINESS OUTCOME
+
CTA


Emails should sound like they were written by a salesperson who understands:

- The seller's company
- The seller's services
- The prospect's company
- The prospect's industry
- The relevant business challenge


Do NOT start emails with:

"I noticed..."
"I came across..."
"I wanted to reach out..."
"I hope this email finds you well."


Instead use a relevant business-value opening.


====================================================
EXAMPLE — JANITORIAL
====================================================

User selects:

Janitorial

Website:

www.whitlockservices.com

iSDRX AI analyzes Whitlock's website and creates the seller profile.

Prospect:

Large warehouse

The system should compare the warehouse's operations to Whitlock's actual
services and differentiators and explain why Whitlock may be relevant.

If another janitorial customer signs up and enters:

www.differentcleaningcompany.com

the SAME Janitorial industry model should analyze that company's website and
use THEIR services, differentiators, capabilities, and value proposition.

Do not continue using Whitlock's value proposition for the new customer.


====================================================
EXAMPLE — FLEET WRAPS
====================================================

User selects:

Fleet & Commercial Vehicle Wraps

Website:

www.goc4wraps.com

Use C4's seller intelligence.

If another wrap business later signs up and enters its own website, use that
company's value proposition automatically.

Do not require a new Fleet Wrap profile or custom code.


====================================================
FALLBACK WHEN WEBSITE IS MISSING
====================================================

Because Company Website is currently optional:

If no website is provided:

Use the generic industry profile and existing industry-based prospect
intelligence.

Do NOT invent seller-specific services or differentiators.

The system may display:

"Add your company website in Settings to improve seller-specific prospect
intelligence and email personalization."


====================================================
FALLBACK WHEN WEBSITE CANNOT BE ANALYZED
====================================================

If the website is unavailable, blocked, invalid, or contains insufficient
information:

Do not fail Prospect Search.

Continue using the generic industry profile.

Clearly mark the seller intelligence status as:

Company Intelligence Limited

Do not fabricate information from the seller's website.


====================================================

- Certifications

- Clients
- Awards
- Guarantees
- Pricing
- ROI
- Case study results
- Customer outcomes

====================================================
IMPORTANT COST / PERFORMANCE REQUIREMENT
====================================================

Website analysis should happen primarily during profile setup or refresh.

Do NOT repeatedly crawl and analyze the seller website for each prospect.

Store the resulting Seller Intelligence Profile and reuse it for:

- Prospect qualification
- Why Target
- Potential Needs
- Opportunity scoring
- Recommended Outreach
- Email personalization
- Campaign creation

This architecture is required to minimize unnecessary AI/API costs and
improve response speed.
