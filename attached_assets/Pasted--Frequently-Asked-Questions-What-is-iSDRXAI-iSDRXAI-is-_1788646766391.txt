## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is iSDRXAI?

iSDRXAI is an AI-powered Sales Development Representative that helps businesses research prospects, identify buying signals, create personalized outreach, manage follow-up campaigns, and schedule appointments directly on the sales team’s calendar.

### How does iSDRXAI help my sales team?

iSDRXAI handles time-consuming prospecting activities so your sales professionals can focus on conversations, presentations, and closing business. It helps keep your pipeline active without requiring your team to spend hours researching prospects and writing individual emails.

### Does iSDRXAI replace a salesperson?

No. iSDRXAI supports your sales team by handling many of the repetitive tasks normally performed at the beginning of the sales process. Your sales professionals remain responsible for building relationships, conducting discovery meetings, presenting solutions, and closing opportunities.

### Can iSDRXAI help if we have an open sales position?

Yes. iSDRXAI can begin prospecting and generating engagement while you recruit, hire, and train a new Sales Development Representative or Business Development Representative. It can also continue supporting the new employee after the position is filled.

### What types of businesses can use iSDRXAI?

iSDRXAI can be customized for many business-to-business industries, including:

* Commercial cleaning and facility services
* IT services and managed service providers
* Property management
* Healthcare
* Manufacturing
* Professional services
* Commercial fleet graphics and vehicle wraps
* Construction and trade services

### How does iSDRXAI find potential customers?

iSDRXAI uses your ideal customer profile, target industries, geographic market, company characteristics, job openings, leadership information, and other relevant business signals to help identify companies that may be a good fit for your services.

### What are buying signals?

Buying signals are activities or business changes that may indicate a company has a potential need. Examples include hiring activity, company growth, leadership changes, new locations, operational challenges, expansion announcements, or increased engagement with your outreach.

### Does iSDRXAI personalize every email?

Yes. iSDRXAI uses available information about the prospect, company, industry, and possible business needs to create relevant one-to-one messages. The emails are designed to sound professional and natural instead of reading like generic mass marketing.

### Can I review the emails before a campaign begins?

Yes. You can preview the email style, messaging, offer, call to action, signature, logo, and supporting attachments during campaign preparation. Once the campaign is approved and activated, iSDRXAI can automate the outreach sequence.

### Does iSDRXAI send follow-up emails?

Yes. iSDRXAI can support structured multi-step campaigns that include the initial introduction, follow-up messages, helpful resources, case studies, videos, and a final breakup email.

### Can prospects schedule appointments through iSDRXAI?

Yes. Your calendar link can be included in the outreach so interested prospects can schedule an appointment directly with the appropriate sales professional.

### How does iSDRXAI determine which prospects are most engaged?

iSDRXAI monitors engagement activity such as email opens, repeat opens, link clicks, website visits, attachment views, video engagement, and calendar-link activity. These signals help identify hot, warm, and lower-engagement prospects for follow-up.

### Will iSDRXAI automatically close sales for my company?

No. iSDRXAI is designed to improve prospecting, engagement, and appointment generation. Sales results depend on several factors, including your offer, market demand, pricing, sales process, follow-up, and the ability of your team to convert opportunities.

### How quickly can we get started?

### What does the setup process include?

Setup may include:

* Defining your ideal customer profile
* Selecting industries, locations, and decision-makers
* Developing campaign messaging and scripts
* Configuring your CRM and calendar
* Preparing email sequences and campaign materials
* Testing campaign workflows
* Training your team
* Preparing the campaign for launch
