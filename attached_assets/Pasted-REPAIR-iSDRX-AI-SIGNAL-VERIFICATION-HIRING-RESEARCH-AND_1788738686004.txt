=========================================================
PRIMARY PROBLEM
=========================================================

The current system is sometimes treating basic company verification data as
a buying signal.

Example:

Google Places verifies:
- company exists
- operating address
- website
- phone

The system then incorrectly moves from:

"Potential need"

to language such as:

"confirmed need"

even though no actual fleet, vehicle, branding, expansion, hiring, or other
company-specific buying need has been verified.

THIS MUST BE CORRECTED.


=========================================================
1. SEPARATE COMPANY VERIFICATION FROM BUYING SIGNALS
=========================================================

Create a clear distinction between:

A. COMPANY VERIFICATION
B. VERIFIED BUYING SIGNAL
C. POTENTIAL IMPLICATION
D. INDUSTRY / BUSINESS MODEL FIT
E. UNCONFIRMED INFORMATION


-------------------------------------------
A. COMPANY VERIFICATION
-------------------------------------------

Examples:

✓ Active Google Places listing
✓ Public operating address
✓ Public website
✓ Published phone number
✓ Company appears operational
✓ Company industry/category verified

These facts help establish:

DATA QUALITY
COMPANY VALIDITY
CONTACTABILITY

They are NOT buying signals by themselves.

Do NOT award Verified Buying Signal points simply because:

- Google Places returned the company
- the company has a website
- the company has an address
- the company has a phone number


=========================================================
2. VERIFIED BUYING SIGNAL DEFINITION
=========================================================

A VERIFIED BUYING SIGNAL requires company-specific evidence that could
reasonably affect the need for the seller's service.

Examples include:

- Hiring employees
- Hiring field technicians
- Hiring drivers
- Hiring installers
- Hiring sales representatives who travel
- Opening a new location
- Expanding into a new territory
- Acquiring another company
- Announcing business expansion
- Purchasing vehicles
- Adding fleet vehicles
- Rebranding
- New logo / brand launch
- Facility opening
- Facility expansion
- Construction project
- Contract award
- RFP / procurement activity
- Leadership change where relevant
- New service line
- Franchise expansion
- Funding/growth event where relevant
- Relevant compliance or technology initiative

A verified signal MUST have supporting evidence.


=========================================================
3. ADD HIRING RESEARCH AS A CORE SIGNAL
=========================================================

Every prospect research process should actively investigate whether the
company is currently hiring or has recently been hiring.

DO NOT only look for general company information.

Research:

- Company careers page
- Public job listings
- Search-indexed job postings
- LinkedIn/public hiring information where accessible
- Applicant tracking system pages
- Indeed/public job pages where available
- Company announcements referencing hiring
- News/press releases mentioning workforce expansion
- Other reliable public hiring sources already supported by the application


Capture:

hiringStatus
hiringRoles[]
hiringRoleCount
hiringDepartments[]
hiringLocations[]
hiringSource
hiringSourceURL
hiringDate
hiringConfidence
hiringEvidence


Possible hiringStatus values:

VERIFIED_ACTIVE_HIRING
RECENT_HIRING_SIGNAL
NO_ACTIVE_HIRING_FOUND
UNKNOWN


IMPORTANT:

"No jobs found" does NOT mean:

"Company is not hiring."

It means:

"No active hiring signal was found from the sources searched."

=========================================================
5. HIRING RECENCY
=========================================================

Track freshness.

Suggested classification:

0–30 days:
VERY RECENT

31–90 days:
RECENT

91–180 days:
ACTIVE / MODERATELY RECENT

181–365 days:
OLDER

365+ days:
STALE


Recent hiring signals should be weighted more strongly than older hiring
information.

Never describe a stale posting as:

"currently hiring"

unless it is still demonstrably active.


=========================================================
6. SOURCE QUALITY
=========================================================

Rank hiring evidence.

HIGH CONFIDENCE:

- Official company careers page
- Company's ATS/job application page
- Official company press release
- Official public-sector job posting
- Verified company announcement

MEDIUM CONFIDENCE:

- Major established job board showing an active listing
- Reputable business/news source
- Multiple corroborating sources

LOW CONFIDENCE:

- Old cached result
- Aggregated listing with no current validation
- Search snippet with uncertain status


Low-confidence hiring information should NOT be represented as a definitive
current hiring signal.


=========================================================
7. FIX "CONFIRMED NEED" LANGUAGE
=========================================================

Search the existing application for logic or prompts that produce phrases
such as:

"confirmed need"

"confirmed need around"

"the company needs"

"they need"

"requires"

"is expanding"

"is growing its fleet"

"is replacing"

when those statements are based only on inference.

Remove or correct this behavior.


A need can only be described as CONFIRMED if there is direct supporting
evidence.

Otherwise use:

- Potential need
- Potential opportunity
- May create a need
- Could become relevant
- May indicate
- If confirmed during discovery
- Could support
- May benefit from
- Appears potentially relevant


=========================================================
8. FIX POTENTIAL NEEDS SECTION
=========================================================

The current Potential Needs section sometimes converts an inferred
opportunity into a confirmed requirement.

BAD EXAMPLE:

Potential opportunity: vehicle identification →
C4 Wraps' custom design and installation may address the confirmed need →
Mobile billboards for branding and marketing.


There is NO confirmed need.


CORRECT FORMAT:

Potential opportunity:
Vehicle identification

Why it may matter:
If the prospect operates customer-facing commercial vehicles, consistent
vehicle identification could support brand visibility and professionalism.

Relevant seller capability:
C4 Wraps — commercial vehicle wrap design and installation.

Business value:
Potentially stronger local brand visibility and fleet consistency.

Status:
UNCONFIRMED — vehicle ownership and current wrap requirements have not yet
been verified.

=========================================================
29. FINAL QUALITY CONTROL
=========================================================

Before displaying prospect intelligence, run a validation check:

For every statement, ask:

1. Is this company verification or a buying signal?
2. Is there supporting evidence?
3. Is the evidence current enough?
4. Is this a fact or implication?
5. Is the language accurately labeled?
6. Did an implication accidentally become a "confirmed need"?
7. Is a seller capability being mistaken for a prospect need?
8. Is hiring actually relevant to this seller's industry?
9. Are duplicate job listings inflating the signal?
10. Would a salesperson reading this mistakenly believe something was
    verified when it was not?

If yes, correct the language/classification before rendering.
