AUTOMATED GIFT OUTREACH SETTINGS

Add a new Settings section named:

“Professional Gift Outreach”

This section controls the optional gift and package follow-up steps across the
entire automated outreach campaign.

MASTER SETTING

Professional Gift Outreach:
- Enabled
- Disabled

When enabled:
- Convert the standard seven-step campaign into an eight-step campaign.
- Add the Professional Gift action as Step 2.
- Add the Package Follow-Up as Step 3.
- Shift the remaining campaign steps forward.
- Preserve all existing campaign content and delays.

When disabled:
- Use the standard seven-step campaign.

STEP 3 FOLLOW-UP METHOD

Allow the user to select a default:

How would you like to follow up after sending a gift?
- AI-Written Email
- Personalized MP4 Video Email
- Choose Separately for Each Prospect

OPTION 1: AI-WRITTEN EMAIL

Automatically generate a personalized package follow-up using:
- Prospect’s preferred name
- Prospect’s company
- Gift sent
- Date sent
- User’s selected industry
- Industry-specific operational priorities
- User’s title and company
- Scheduling link

Use the approved Step 3 tone:
- Warm
- Conversational
- Confident
- Professionally personalized
- Focused on moving the conversation forward
- Not repetitive of the original gift note
- Low-pressure discovery-call request

Approved structure:

Subject: Did the [Gift Name] arrive?

Hi [Preferred Name],

I wanted to check that the package I sent on [Date Sent] made it to you safely.
I hope you’re enjoying the [Gift Name] and that it brings [short personalized
statement connected to the gift].

I would like to learn more about how [Prospect Company] currently manages
[industry-relevant area]. [Brief explanation of two or three operational
priorities.] Are any of those areas top of mind for you right now?

I’d be glad to learn how you’re currently managing those priorities and share
how we may be able to help. Would you be open to a quick conversation? You can
choose a time that works best for you here: [Scheduling Link].

Best,

[User Title]
[User Company]

OPTION 2: PERSONALIZED MP4 VIDEO EMAIL

Allow the user to upload an MP4 video for the individual prospect.

Required fields:
- MP4 video upload
- Video title
- Prospect name
- Gift referenced
- Date gift was sent
- Written call-to-action
- Scheduling link

Optional fields:
- Custom thumbnail
- Video expiration date
- Short written introduction
- Captions or transcript

Display the uploaded video inside the email as:
- A personalized thumbnail with a play button
- Prospect’s name on the thumbnail, when selected
- A secure link to a mobile-friendly video landing page
- A written call-to-action beneath the video
- A scheduling button

Do not attach a large MP4 file directly to the email. Host the video securely
and place a clickable thumbnail in the email to improve deliverability.

Suggested video email text:

Subject: Did the [Gift Name] arrive?

Hi [Preferred Name],

I wanted to make sure the package I sent on [Date Sent] reached you safely. I
recorded this short message for you.

[PERSONALIZED VIDEO THUMBNAIL]

I’d welcome an opportunity to learn more about your current priorities and
explore whether we may be able to help.

[WATCH VIDEO]
[SCHEDULE A QUICK CONVERSATION]

Best,

[User Title]
[User Company]

AUTOMATION TIMING

Schedule Step 3 using this priority:

1. Carrier confirms delivery:
   Send on the next business day.

2. User enters an expected delivery date:
   Send one business day after the expected delivery date.

3. Only the sent date is available:
   Send three to five business days after the sent date, based on the user’s
   Settings preference.

SETTINGS FIELDS

Add:
- Default follow-up method
- Default number of business days after delivery
- User’s selected industry
- Service descriptions
- Industry-specific operational discussion points
- Default scheduling link
- Email signature
- Video hosting preference
- Default video call-to-action
- Business hours
- Sending time zone
- Allowed sending days
- Automatic sending or approval required
- Email fallback if the video is unavailable
- Maximum follow-up attempts

AUTOMATION LEVEL

Allow the user to select:

1. Fully Automated
   AI prepares and sends the follow-up automatically according to the campaign
   schedule.

2. Review Before Sending
   AI prepares the email or video email and requires user approval.

3. Manual
   AI prepares the content, but the user chooses when to send it.

DEFAULT RECOMMENDATION

Set “Review Before Sending” as the default until the user intentionally enables
Fully Automated.

GIFT CONFIRMATION REQUIREMENT

Step 3 cannot be scheduled until:

Gift sent? = Yes

When Yes is selected, require:
- Date sent
- Gift name

Also collect when available:
- Expected delivery date
- Carrier
- Tracking number or link
- Amount spent
- Gift note used

If Gift sent? = No:
- Do not send the package follow-up.
- Keep the record at Step 2.
- Allow the user to mark the gift as sent or skip the step later.

VIDEO TRACKING

Track:
- Video email delivered
- Thumbnail clicked
- Video started
- 25%, 50%, 75% and 100% viewed
- Scheduling link clicked
- Reply received
- Discovery call booked

EMAIL TRACKING

Track:
- Email delivered
- Email opened
- Links clicked
- Reply received
- Discovery call booked

FAILSAFE RULES

- Never say the package was received unless delivery is confirmed.
- Ask whether it arrived safely.
- Do not send Step 3 before the expected delivery date.
- Do not repeat the original gift note.
- Do not send an email and video email simultaneously.
- If an MP4 is missing or fails processing, pause the step or use the approved
  email fallback based on Settings.
- Prevent duplicate follow-ups.
- Stop automated follow-ups when the prospect replies or books a meeting.
- Require user approval before sending if gift-policy or compliance risks are
  detected.
