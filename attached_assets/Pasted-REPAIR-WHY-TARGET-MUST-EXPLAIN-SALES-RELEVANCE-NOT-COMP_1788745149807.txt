REPAIR — WHY TARGET MUST EXPLAIN SALES RELEVANCE, NOT COMPANY VERIFICATION

There is still a logic problem in Prospect Cards.

Current incorrect example:

"Why target: Verified: Vonlane Houston was returned by Google Places for the
selected Local business search in Houston, TX; a public operating address is
listed; a public website is listed."

THIS IS NOT A REASON TO TARGET THE COMPANY.

Google Places, public address, website, and phone are COMPANY VERIFICATION
information.

They should NEVER be used as the primary content of WHY TARGET.


========================================================
1. DEFINE THE PURPOSE OF "WHY TARGET"
========================================================

WHY TARGET must answer:

"Why should this seller spend sales time pursuing this company?"

It should explain:

- Why the prospect fits the seller's ICP
- Why the prospect's business model is relevant
- Which verified signals make the account timely
- Which seller capabilities align with the prospect
- Why the opportunity may be worth pursuing

WHY TARGET should NOT simply prove that the company exists.


========================================================
2. MOVE GOOGLE PLACES INFORMATION
========================================================

Move information such as:

- Google Places returned the company
- Public operating address
- Public website
- Published phone
- Business listing verified

into:

VERIFIED COMPANY INFORMATION

Example:

VERIFIED COMPANY INFORMATION

✓ Active business listing
✓ Houston, TX operating location
✓ Public website
✓ Published business phone


Do NOT display this as WHY TARGET.


========================================================
3. WHY TARGET PRIORITY ORDER
========================================================

Generate WHY TARGET using this priority:

1. VERIFIED BUYING SIGNALS
2. VERIFIED BUSINESS MODEL RELEVANCE
3. SELLER-TO-PROSPECT MATCH
4. INDUSTRY / ICP FIT
5. POTENTIAL BUSINESS NEED
6. COMPANY VERIFICATION — supporting only, never primary


Preferred reasoning:

VERIFIED SIGNAL
+
BUSINESS MODEL
+
SELLER CAPABILITY
+
POTENTIAL BUSINESS VALUE
=
WHY TARGET


========================================================
4. EXAMPLE — VONLANE
========================================================

For a company like Vonlane, do NOT write:

"Why target: Google Places verified Vonlane Houston..."


Instead, research and reason about:

- Is transportation a core part of the business?
- Are vehicles central to delivering the service?
- Is the fleet customer-facing?
- Is brand presentation important?
- Are there multiple vehicles or routes?
- Is the company hiring drivers?
- Is the company adding routes?
- Is it entering new markets?
- Is it adding vehicles?
- Is it rebranding?
- Is there evidence of fleet expansion?
- Are there new service locations?
- Are there company announcements relevant to fleet operations?


If verified evidence supports only the business model, an acceptable WHY TARGET
would be:

"Vonlane operates a customer-facing transportation business where vehicles
are central to the customer experience. That creates a potentially strong
fit for C4 Wraps' commercial vehicle graphics and fleet-brand consistency
capabilities. No current vehicle purchase, fleet expansion, rebrand, or other
time-sensitive buying signal has been verified."


This is useful even without a current buying signal because it explains the
ICP fit while remaining accurate.


========================================================
5. IF A VERIFIED HIRING SIGNAL EXISTS
========================================================

The system MUST research current hiring.

For fleet prospects, specifically look for:

- Drivers
- CDL Drivers
- Chauffeurs
- Fleet personnel
- Mechanics
- Field technicians
- Service technicians
- Installers
- Delivery employees
- Route personnel
- Mobile service employees
- Operations roles tied to fleet growth


Example:

VERIFIED SIGNAL:
Vonlane is actively recruiting drivers in Houston and Dallas.

WHY TARGET:

"Vonlane's transportation model already depends on customer-facing vehicles,
and current driver recruiting may indicate continued demand for operating
capacity. C4 Wraps' fleet branding capabilities could become relevant as
vehicles are added, replaced, or reassigned. Additional vehicle purchases have
not been verified."


IMPORTANT:

Hiring drivers does NOT automatically mean:

"Vonlane is buying more vehicles."

It means:

"Current driver recruiting may indicate increased or sustained operating
capacity."

Vehicle growth remains unconfirmed unless verified separately.


========================================================
6. IF A VERIFIED EXPANSION SIGNAL EXISTS
========================================================

Example:

VERIFIED:
New Houston-to-Austin route announced.

WHY TARGET:

"Vonlane recently announced an additional Texas route, creating a timely
reason to evaluate fleet-brand consistency as service coverage expands.
Because C4 Wraps specializes in commercial vehicle graphics and consistent
fleet branding, the expansion creates a relevant sales opportunity."

Only say this if the route announcement is actually verified.


========================================================
7. IF NO STRONG BUYING SIGNAL EXISTS
========================================================

Do NOT fall back to Google Places.

Instead say:

WHY TARGET:

"This company fits the target profile because its business model relies on
customer-facing commercial vehicles, making fleet branding potentially
relevant. No strong company-specific buying signal has been verified yet."


Or, if even business-model fit is weak:

WHY TARGET:

"No strong seller-specific reason to prioritize this prospect has been
identified yet."


This is acceptable.

The AI employee does NOT need to manufacture a reason to target every company.


========================================================
8. ALLOW "LOW PRIORITY" PROSPECTS
========================================================

The system should be willing to conclude:

LOW PRIORITY

or

WEAK SIGNAL FIT


Do not force every Google Places company to look like a qualified prospect.

If the company:

- Has no verified buying signal
- Has weak seller relevance
- Has no relevant business-model connection
- Has no useful decision maker
- Has no hiring/expansion signal

then Opportunity Score should remain low and WHY TARGET should explain that.


========================================================
9. WHY TARGET STRUCTURE
========================================================

Use this internal format:

FIT:
Why does this company's business model align with the seller?

SIGNAL:
What current verified event makes the account timely?

SELLER MATCH:
Which seller capability is relevant?

VALUE:
What potential business outcome could the seller support?


Then generate a concise human-readable WHY TARGET.


Example:

FIT:
Vonlane operates customer-facing commercial vehicles.

SIGNAL:
No current expansion signal verified.

SELLER MATCH:
C4 Wraps provides fleet graphics and commercial vehicle branding.

VALUE:
Consistent fleet branding and brand visibility.


Rendered:

WHY TARGET:

"Vonlane operates a customer-facing transportation business where vehicle
branding is directly connected to brand visibility and customer experience.
That creates a strong business-model fit for C4 Wraps' fleet graphics
capabilities, although no current expansion, vehicle purchase, or rebranding
signal has been verified."


========================================================
10. POTENTIAL NEEDS MUST FOLLOW WHY TARGET
========================================================

Potential Needs should derive from legitimate fit/signals.

For Vonlane, appropriate potential needs might include:

POTENTIAL OPPORTUNITY:
Fleet brand consistency

Relevant capability:
C4 Wraps commercial fleet graphics

Potential value:
Maintain consistent presentation across customer-facing vehicles

Status:
Potential — current wrap requirements have not been verified.


Another:

POTENTIAL OPPORTUNITY:
Replacement vehicle graphics

Relevant capability:
Commercial vehicle wrap design and installation

Potential value:
Maintain consistent branding when vehicles are replaced

Status:
Unconfirmed — no vehicle replacement activity has been verified.


========================================================
11. RECOMMENDED OUTREACH SHOULD BE STRATEGIC
========================================================

Current:

"No strong company-specific buying signal has been verified. Use
discovery-led messaging..."


This is acceptable but too generic.


Improve to:

"Lead with the importance of maintaining consistent fleet presentation in a
premium customer-facing transportation business. Use discovery-oriented
messaging because no current fleet expansion or rebranding event has been
verified."


Recommended Outreach should answer:

WHAT SHOULD I TALK ABOUT?

not merely:

WHAT SHOULD I AVOID SAYING?


========================================================
12. RESEARCH HIRING BEFORE FINALIZING WHY TARGET
========================================================

Before rendering WHY TARGET, attempt to identify:

- current job openings
- field/mobile employee hiring
- driver hiring
- technician hiring
- operations hiring
- expansion-related hiring


For Fleet & Commercial Vehicle Wraps, hiring should be a formal research step.


Use:

Company careers page
Job boards supported by existing system
ATS pages
Public company posts
Press releases
Search-indexed postings


Capture:

verifiedHiringRoles[]
hiringLocations[]
hiringFreshness
hiringSource
hiringSourceURL
hiringConfidence


Do not invent counts.


========================================================
13. RESEARCH EXPANSION SIGNALS
========================================================

Before finalizing WHY TARGET, also look for:

- new routes
- new territories
- new locations
- acquisitions
- franchise expansion
- vehicle purchases
- new fleet announcements
- service expansion
- new facilities
- rebranding
- logo changes
- leadership announcements related to expansion


These are much more valuable than Google Places verification.

========================================================
15. OPPORTUNITY SCORE CONNECTION
========================================================

Google Places verification should not materially raise Opportunity Score.

Example:

Google Places verified business:
Company validity/data quality points only.


Transportation business using vehicles:
Industry/business-model fit points.


Hiring drivers:
Verified Buying Signal points.


Adding routes:
Verified Buying Signal points.


Confirmed vehicle purchase:
Very strong Verified Buying Signal points.


Strong match to seller services:
Seller Match points.


The score must reflect sales opportunity, not merely existence.


========================================================
16. QUALITY CHECK
========================================================

Before rendering WHY TARGET, ask:

"If I removed the company name, could this sentence apply to almost every
company returned by Google Places?"

If YES:

Regenerate WHY TARGET.


Also ask:

"Does this explain why this particular seller should pursue this particular
company?"

If NO:

Regenerate.


And:

"Is the reason based primarily on address, website, phone, or Google Places?"

If YES:

Move that information to VERIFIED COMPANY INFORMATION and regenerate WHY
TARGET.


========================================================
17. CRITICAL PRINCIPLE
========================================================

GOOGLE PLACES ANSWERS:

"Is this a real/operating company?"


WHY TARGET ANSWERS:

"Why is this potentially a good sales opportunity for THIS seller?"


These are NOT the same question.
