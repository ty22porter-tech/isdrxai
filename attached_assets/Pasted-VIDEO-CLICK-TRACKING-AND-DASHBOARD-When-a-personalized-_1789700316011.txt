VIDEO CLICK TRACKING AND DASHBOARD

When a personalized video email is sent, route the video thumbnail and
“Watch Video” button through a unique tracked link assigned to:

- Prospect ID
- Campaign ID
- Campaign step
- Email ID
- Video ID

DASHBOARD STATUS

Display one of these statuses:

- Video Email Scheduled
- Video Email Sent
- Video Email Delivered
- Email Opened
- Video Not Clicked
- Video Clicked
- Video Started
- Video Partially Watched
- Video Completed
- Scheduling Link Clicked
- Discovery Call Booked

VIDEO CLICK EVENT

When the prospect clicks the video thumbnail or Watch Video button:

1. Record “Video Clicked.”
2. Capture the first-click date and time.
3. Capture the most recent click date and time.
4. Count total clicks.
5. Distinguish unique clicks from repeated clicks.
6. Update the prospect’s campaign timeline.
7. Display the activity on the dashboard immediately.
8. Notify the assigned user.
9. Add the appropriate engagement-score points.

Recommended engagement scoring:

- Email opened: +1
- Email opened more than once: +2 total
- Video clicked: +10
- Video watched at least 50%: +5 additional
- Video watched completely: +10 additional
- Scheduling link clicked: +10
- Discovery call booked: Mark as Hot/Meeting Booked

Do not award video-click points multiple times for repeated clicks. Repeated
activity should still appear in the timeline.

IMPORTANT TRACKING DISTINCTION

Track these as separate events:

1. Email opened
2. Video thumbnail clicked
3. Video playback started
4. Watch percentage
5. Scheduling link clicked

A video click must not be recorded merely because the email was opened.

If the MP4 is hosted on a player that supports playback analytics, track:
- Video started
- 25% watched
- 50% watched
- 75% watched
- 100% watched

If the hosting provider does not support playback analytics, track the thumbnail
or Watch Video link click and display:

“Video Clicked — Viewing Completion Unavailable.”
