Update the Prospect Search feature in the iSDRX AI app so that prospect results, search logic, filters, AI research, and recommendations change based on the selected business vertical.

The platform currently has two verticals:

Janitorial Services
IT Services
Problem to Fix

When the user switches from Janitorial Services to IT Services, the app continues displaying prospects generated from the Janitorial Services search criteria.

This must be corrected.

Prospect data, AI research, search parameters, recommendations, scoring, and displayed results must be isolated by vertical. Janitorial prospects must never appear in the IT Services prospect list unless the company independently qualifies under the IT Services criteria.

Vertical-Switching Behavior

When the user changes the selected vertical:

Clear the currently displayed prospect list.
Clear the previous vertical’s filters.
Clear cached AI research results associated with the previous vertical.
Reset pagination and selected prospects.
Load the saved filters for the newly selected vertical, when available.
Run a new search using the newly selected vertical’s targeting criteria.
Display a loading message:

Building your IT Services prospect list…

Do not reuse the previous vertical’s search query or prospect results.

Store the selected vertical with every prospect search and every prospect record.

Example:

vertical: "janitorial"

or

vertical: "it_services"

Every backend request, database query, saved list, campaign, export, and AI research result must include the vertical identifier.

IT Prospect Scoring

Create an IT Opportunity Score from 0–100.

Use these scoring factors:

Factor	Maximum Points
Employee count within target range	15
Compliance-sensitive industry	20
Multi-location organization	10
Likely managed IT need	15
Likely cybersecurity need	20
Relevant decision-maker identified	10
Recent growth or technology signal	10
Score Labels
80–100: High Priority
60–79: Strong Fit
40–59: Possible Fit
Below 40: Low Priority

Display the score and a short explanation.

Example:

IT Opportunity Score: 86 — High Priority
Healthcare organization with 125 employees, multiple locations, likely HIPAA-related security needs, and an identified IT Director.

Do not invent facts. Clearly label inferred information.

AI Research Instructions for IT Services

When researching an IT Services prospect, instruct the AI to determine:

What the organization does.
Its estimated employee count.
Whether it has multiple locations.
Whether it operates in a compliance-sensitive industry.
Whether an internal IT department is visible.
Which managed IT or cybersecurity services may be relevant.
Which decision-maker should be contacted.
What business signal makes the company worth approaching.
A personalized outreach angle.
Whether the information is confirmed or inferred.

The AI must not reuse Janitorial research prompts, Janitorial pain points, cleaning terminology, facility-cleaning services, walkthrough language, or cleaning-service recommendations when IT Services is selected.
