====================================================
AI EMAIL GENERATION — SELLER VALUE MATCHING
====================================================

CRITICAL REQUIREMENT:

ALL AI-generated prospecting emails must use the Seller Intelligence Profile
created from the Company Website entered in Settings.

The Company Website is not only for the user's email signature.

It is a primary intelligence source that teaches iSDRX AI:

- What the user's company does
- What it sells
- What services it provides
- What problems it solves
- Its strongest differentiators
- Its certifications
- Its areas of expertise
- Industries it serves
- Business outcomes it can provide
- Relevant proof points
- Portfolio/case-study experience where available
- How it positions itself in the market

Use this intelligence to determine WHY the seller is relevant to each
individual prospect.


====================================================
EMAIL VALUE-MATCHING PROCESS
====================================================

Before generating an outreach email, perform this reasoning:

SELLER INTELLIGENCE
        +
PROSPECT COMPANY RESEARCH
        +
VERIFIED PROSPECT SIGNALS
        ↓
IDENTIFY RELEVANT BUSINESS CHALLENGE / OPPORTUNITY
        ↓
SELECT MOST RELEVANT SELLER CAPABILITY
        ↓
SELECT MOST RELEVANT SELLER DIFFERENTIATOR
        ↓
IDENTIFY POTENTIAL BUSINESS VALUE
        ↓
DETERMINE BEST SALES ANGLE
        ↓
WRITE PERSONALIZED EMAIL
        ↓
CREATE ENGAGEMENT-FOCUSED CTA


The email should NOT attempt to communicate every service the seller offers.

Select the 1–2 seller capabilities that are MOST relevant to the specific
prospect and signal.


====================================================
PROSPECT SIGNAL → SELLER VALUE
====================================================

The AI must compare prospect signal intelligence directly against the
seller's capabilities.

Examples:

Prospect Signal:
Company is hiring additional HVAC technicians.

Seller Website:
Seller specializes in commercial fleet wraps and consistent fleet branding.

Email Angle:
As additional technicians and service vehicles are added, maintaining
consistent vehicle branding can help the company extend its local visibility
and present a professional brand across its service territory.


Prospect Signal:
Company is opening a new distribution facility.

Seller Website:
Seller specializes in industrial janitorial and warehouse cleaning.

Email Angle:
A new distribution operation creates an opportunity to establish a cleaning
program around warehouse traffic, floors, employee areas, restrooms and
operating schedules before routines become difficult to change.


Prospect Signal:
Healthcare organization is expanding locations.

Seller Website:
IT provider specializes in cybersecurity, compliance and multi-location
managed IT.

Email Angle:
Expansion can increase the number of endpoints, users, locations and systems
an IT team must secure and support. Position the seller's relevant managed IT
and cybersecurity capabilities around supporting that growth.


====================================================
EMAIL OBJECTIVE
====================================================

The primary purpose of every email is to CREATE ENGAGEMENT.

The email should make the recipient think:

"This is relevant to what our business is doing."

NOT:

"This company is trying to sell me something."

Emails should be:

- Relevant
- Concise
- Professional
- Human
- Consultative
- Business-outcome focused
- Specific enough to demonstrate research
- Easy to respond to

Avoid sounding overly promotional or AI-generated.


====================================================
EMAIL OPENING
====================================================

Do NOT begin emails with:

"I noticed..."
"I came across..."
"I wanted to reach out..."
"I hope this email finds you well..."
"My name is..."
"We are a leading..."
"Are you looking for..."

Do not begin by introducing the seller.

Start with the PROSPECT'S business situation, challenge, opportunity or
relevant business outcome.

The opening should give the reader a reason to continue reading.


Examples:

Fleet Wraps:

"Every service vehicle your technicians put on the road can create additional
brand visibility in the same neighborhoods you're already serving."


Janitorial:

"Keeping a growing distribution operation consistently clean without
interrupting daily workflow requires a different approach than standard
office cleaning."


IT Services:

"Adding locations can quickly increase the number of users, endpoints and
systems an IT team has to secure and support."


These are examples of the approach, not templates to repeat.


====================================================
EMAIL BODY
====================================================

After establishing relevance, connect the prospect's situation to the
seller's specific value proposition.

Use:

PROSPECT SITUATION
→
BUSINESS CHALLENGE
→
SELLER CAPABILITY
→
SELLER DIFFERENTIATOR
→
POTENTIAL BUSINESS OUTCOME

Do not dump a list of services.

Do not write paragraphs describing the seller's entire company.

Explain only the capabilities that matter to THIS prospect.


====================================================
USE SELLER STRENGTHS INTELLIGENTLY
====================================================

If the Seller Intelligence Profile contains strengths such as:

- Certifications
- Specialized expertise
- Proprietary process
- Industry experience
- Technology
- Quality-control systems
- Warranty
- In-house capabilities
- Fast turnaround
- Multi-location support
- Custom programs
- Dedicated account management
- Case studies
- Portfolio experience

select ONLY the differentiator most relevant to the prospect.

Example:

If a janitorial seller has CIMS certification and the prospect is a large
facility with strong quality-control requirements, CIMS may be highly
relevant.

If the prospect is primarily concerned with post-construction cleaning,
another seller capability may be more important.

Do not force the same differentiator into every email.


====================================================
VERIFIED SIGNALS
====================================================

Use verified prospect signals when available, including:

- Hiring
- New locations
- Expansion
- New facilities
- New contracts
- Acquisitions
- Leadership changes
- Construction
- RFP activity
- Technology initiatives
- Regulatory requirements
- Rebranding
- Fleet growth
- Relevant job openings
- Other industry-specific signals

Signals should provide CONTEXT for the outreach.

Do not simply write:

"I saw that you're hiring."

Instead interpret WHY the signal could create a relevant business challenge.


Example:

Weak:
"I noticed you're hiring HVAC technicians."

Better:
"Adding field technicians can also mean putting more service vehicles into
the market, making consistent fleet branding increasingly important as the
operation grows."


====================================================
WHEN NO STRONG SIGNAL EXISTS
====================================================

Do not fabricate a signal.

Use the prospect's verified business model, industry, facility type, market,
or operational characteristics to establish relevance.

The AI should be able to generate a strong email without pretending that a
trigger event occurred.


====================================================
EMAIL CTA
====================================================

CTAs should encourage ENGAGEMENT rather than immediately pushing for a sale.

Use low-friction, conversational questions.

Examples:

"Would it be worth comparing how you're handling this today?"

"Is this something your team is currently evaluating?"

"Would a quick conversation around your current approach make sense?"

"Would it be useful to see how other organizations are approaching this?"

"Is fleet branding something your team manages internally or with an outside
partner?"

"Would it make sense to compare your current cleaning program against another
approach?"

Vary the CTA based on the prospect and campaign stage.

Do not use the exact same CTA for every email.


====================================================
CAMPAIGN SEQUENCE
====================================================

Apply Seller Intelligence throughout the ENTIRE email campaign.

Do not personalize only Email #1.

For a multi-step campaign:

EMAIL 1:
Lead with the strongest business challenge / value match.

EMAIL 2:
Introduce another relevant seller capability or business outcome.

EMAIL 3:
Use a relevant differentiator, proof point, portfolio example, certification
or industry expertise when available.

EMAIL 4:
Approach the problem from another business angle.

EMAIL 5:
Use relevant educational insight, case study, attachment, video or supporting
content if available.

EMAIL 6:
Short engagement-focused follow-up based on the previous value propositions.

FINAL EMAIL:
Professional breakup email that gives the prospect an easy opportunity to
respond or indicate timing.

Do not generate seven versions of essentially the same email.


====================================================
ENGAGEMENT-BASED FOLLOW-UP
====================================================

Where the existing iSDRX AI engagement system provides information such as:

- Email opened
- Multiple opens
- Website clicked
- Attachment opened
- Video clicked
- Calendar clicked
- Previous response

use that information to help determine the appropriate next outreach message
when the existing application architecture supports it.

Higher engagement should allow the messaging to become more direct while
remaining professional.


====================================================
SELLER-SPECIFIC EMAIL RULE
====================================================

Two customers using the same iSDRX AI industry profile should NOT
automatically receive the same messaging.

Example:

Janitorial Customer A:
Website emphasizes healthcare cleaning, compliance and disinfection.

Janitorial Customer B:
Website emphasizes industrial facilities, warehouses and floor care.

Both use the Janitorial industry profile.

However, their prospect scoring, value matching, recommended outreach and
emails should reflect THEIR individual Seller Intelligence Profiles.

The industry tells iSDRX AI HOW TO PROSPECT.

The company website tells iSDRX AI HOW THIS PARTICULAR CUSTOMER CAN HELP THE
PROSPECT.


====================================================
EMAIL QUALITY CHECK
====================================================

Before accepting an AI-generated email, internally verify:

1. Is this email clearly relevant to this specific prospect?

2. Did it use a verified signal or legitimate business-model connection?

3. Did it connect that situation to an actual seller capability?

4. Did it communicate business VALUE rather than just list services?

5. Did it use an appropriate seller differentiator?

6. Did it avoid unsupported claims?

7. Does the opening make the recipient want to continue reading?

8. Does it sound human rather than AI-generated?

9. Is the CTA easy to answer?

10. Could this same email be sent unchanged to 100 unrelated companies?

If the answer to #10 is YES, the email is not personalized enough and should
be regenerated.


====================================================
CRITICAL COST / PERFORMANCE RULE
====================================================

Do NOT re-analyze the seller's website every time an email is generated.

Use the cached Seller Intelligence Profile created when:

- Company Website is entered
- Company Website changes
- User selects Refresh Company Intelligence

Prospect research + stored Seller Intelligence should be combined during
email generation.

This prevents unnecessary website crawling, AI calls and cost while
maintaining seller-specific personalization.
