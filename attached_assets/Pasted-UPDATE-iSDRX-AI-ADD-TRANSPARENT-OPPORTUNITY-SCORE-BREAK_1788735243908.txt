UPDATE iSDRX AI — ADD TRANSPARENT OPPORTUNITY SCORE BREAKDOWN

IMPORTANT:

Do not replace the existing Opportunity Score.

Extend the current scoring system so users can understand WHY a prospect
received its score.

The objective is to make iSDRX AI's scoring transparent, trustworthy, and
actionable.


====================================================
1. ADD "VIEW SCORE BREAKDOWN"
====================================================

Wherever an Opportunity Score appears, such as:

add a small clickable option:

View Score Breakdown

Keep the primary prospect card clean.

When clicked, open a modal, drawer, popover, or expandable section using the
existing application design patterns.

====================================================
3. CRITICAL SCORE DEFINITION
====================================================

Clearly explain:

"The Opportunity Score measures how strongly this prospect matches your
company, target customer profile, verified signals, and potential business
need. It does not represent the probability that the prospect will make a
purchase."

Do NOT describe:

86 / 100

as:

86% likelihood to buy

unless the application eventually has statistically validated conversion
data capable of supporting such a probability.


====================================================
4. VERIFIED SIGNALS RECEIVE MORE WEIGHT
====================================================

Continue following the Signal Verification system.

Verified signals should receive the strongest scoring weight.

Potential implications may contribute to fit/relevance scoring but should
receive less weight.

Unverified assumptions receive ZERO buying-signal points.

Example:

Verified hiring signal:
Eligible for signal points.

Verified expansion:
Eligible for signal points.

Industry-based potential fleet fit:
May contribute limited fit points.

Unconfirmed fleet expansion:
0 signal points.

Never increase the Opportunity Score because of an invented or unverified
event.


====================================================

====================================================
6. MAKE SCORING SELLER-SPECIFIC
====================================================

Opportunity scoring must use the Seller Intelligence Profile.

The same prospect should NOT necessarily receive the same score for every
iSDRX AI customer.

Calculate:

PROSPECT FIT
+
VERIFIED SIGNALS
+
SELLER INTELLIGENCE
+
SELLER / PROSPECT VALUE MATCH
+
DECISION-MAKER QUALITY
+
SIGNAL FRESHNESS
=
OPPORTUNITY SCORE


Example:

A prospect could score:

86 for Seller A

because Seller A's capabilities strongly match the identified opportunity.

The same prospect could score:

67 for Seller B

because Seller B provides different services or has a weaker value match.

7. USE SERVICE PRIORITIES

Use the existing:

Do not increase buying-signal points unless there is verified supporting
evidence.

====================================================
10. SCORE CONFIDENCE
====================================================

In addition to Opportunity Score, calculate:

Score Confidence

HIGH
MEDIUM
LOW

Score Confidence represents how much reliable evidence supports the score.

Example:

Opportunity Score:
86 — High Priority

Score Confidence:
High

Supported by:
✓ 3 verified signals
✓ Strong company information
✓ Relevant decision-maker
✓ Strong seller match


Another prospect might show:

Opportunity Score:
82 — High Priority

Score Confidence:
Low

Reason:
Strong industry fit but limited verified company-specific signals.


This distinction is important.

A high opportunity score with low confidence should NOT be treated the same
as a high opportunity score supported by multiple verified signals.


====================================================
11. DO NOT CONFUSE OPPORTUNITY SCORE WITH ENGAGEMENT SCORE
====================================================

Maintain a clear distinction between:

OPPORTUNITY SCORE
"Should we pursue this company?"

and

ENGAGEMENT SCORE
"How is this prospect responding to our outreach?"


Opportunity Score is based primarily on:

- ICP fit
- Seller fit
- Verified signals
- Business relevance
- Decision-maker quality
- Signal freshness


Engagement Score is based on:

- Email opens
- Repeat opens
- Website clicks
- Attachment clicks
- Video interactions
- Calendar clicks
- Replies
- Other campaign behavior


Do not combine these into one unexplained score.
