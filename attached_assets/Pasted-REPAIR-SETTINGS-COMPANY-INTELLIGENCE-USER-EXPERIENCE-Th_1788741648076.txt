REPAIR SETTINGS → COMPANY INTELLIGENCE USER EXPERIENCE

The current Company Intelligence setup is confusing.

The Company Website currently appears as optional, the user is shown optional
manual fields that can make it appear that they are responsible for supplying
the intelligence, and there is a "Refresh Company Intelligence" button that
makes it appear the user must manually refresh the website before iSDRX AI can
use the information.

Simplify this experience.

IMPORTANT:

Do not rebuild Seller Intelligence / Company Intelligence.

Inspect the existing implementation and modify the UX and triggering behavior
while preserving:

- Existing Company Intelligence database/profile
- Existing website analysis service
- Existing Seller Intelligence usage
- Prospect matching
- Opportunity scoring
- Recommended Outreach
- AI email personalization
- Industry profiles
- Existing user-entered overrides
- Existing cached intelligence


========================================================
1. COMPANY WEBSITE MUST BE REQUIRED
========================================================

Change:

Company Website (optional)

to:

Company Website *

or:

Company Website
Required


Helper text:

"iSDRX AI uses your website to learn what your company sells, the industries
you serve, your strengths, differentiators, certifications, capabilities,
and value proposition. This intelligence is used to research prospects and
personalize your sales strategy and outreach."


The website should still be available for the user's email signature where
appropriate, but that should NOT be the primary explanation for this field.


Remove the current helper text as the main description:

"Shown as 'Website' in your email signature when provided."


Replace it with the Company Intelligence explanation above.

Optionally show a smaller secondary note:

"Your website can also be included in your email signature."


========================================================
2. VALID WEBSITE REQUIRED
========================================================

A valid company website must be entered before Company Intelligence can become
Ready.

Do not allow a blank website to produce:

"Company Intelligence Ready."


If the field is empty, display:

COMPANY WEBSITE REQUIRED

"Add your company website so iSDRX AI can learn your business and personalize
prospecting."


If the URL is invalid, display an inline validation message.

Example:

"Enter a valid company website, such as https://yourcompany.com"


========================================================
3. AUTOMATIC WEBSITE ANALYSIS
========================================================

The user should NOT have to manually click Refresh Company Intelligence for
the system to begin using their website.

Automatically analyze the company website when:

1. A valid website is entered for the first time and Settings are saved.

OR

2. The Company Website value changes and Settings are saved.


Workflow:

USER ENTERS WEBSITE
        ↓
SAVE SETTINGS
        ↓
SYSTEM DETECTS NEW / CHANGED WEBSITE
        ↓
AUTOMATICALLY ANALYZE WEBSITE
        ↓
CREATE / UPDATE COMPANY INTELLIGENCE
        ↓
CACHE RESULTS
        ↓
MARK COMPANY INTELLIGENCE READY
        ↓
USE INTELLIGENCE THROUGHOUT iSDRX AI


Do not require another click after Save.


========================================================
4. USE CACHED COMPANY INTELLIGENCE AUTOMATICALLY
========================================================

Once Company Intelligence has been generated successfully, automatically use
the saved/cached intelligence throughout the application.

The user should NOT need to refresh it every time they:

- search for prospects
- generate leads
- create campaigns
- generate emails
- review Opportunity Scores
- view Recommended Outreach
- return to the Settings page
- log into the application


Company Intelligence should remain active until:

- the website changes
- the user edits Company Intelligence
- the user manually requests a reanalysis


========================================================
5. RENAME "REFRESH COMPANY INTELLIGENCE"
========================================================

The current wording is confusing because "Refresh" can imply the user must
press the button before the data becomes active.

Rename:

Refresh Company Intelligence

to:

Reanalyze Website


Tooltip/helper:

"Scan your website again if your services, messaging, capabilities, or company
information have changed."


This is an OPTIONAL maintenance action.

The normal customer should not need to click it after entering their website.


========================================================
6. CLARIFY THE TWO OPTIONAL FIELDS
========================================================

The current fields are useful, but they should clearly supplement the website.

Do NOT make them required.


Change:

"What makes your company different?"

to:

"Anything you want your AI employee to know?"

Optional


Helper:

"Optional: Add strengths, guarantees, processes, certifications, experience,
or advantages that may not be clearly explained on your website."


Change:

"What services or solutions do you want iSDRX AI to prioritize?"

to:

"Services you want your AI employee to prioritize"

Optional


Helper:

"Optional: Tell your AI employee which services or solutions you most want to
sell. If left blank, iSDRX AI will use the services identified from your
website."


This makes it clear that:

THE WEBSITE PROVIDES THE INTELLIGENCE

and

THE USER CAN ADD INSTRUCTIONS IF DESIRED.

website information inside Settings.

- Commercial Vehicle Wraps

========================================================
19. FIRST-TIME SETUP EXPERIENCE
========================================================

For a new account:

1. Select industry
2. Enter Company Website
3. Save
4. Automatically analyze company
5. Show progress
6. Generate Company Intelligence
7. Mark Ready
8. Allow review/edit
9. Begin using intelligence automatically


Do not require the customer to understand AI architecture or perform a
separate refresh action.


========================================================
20. EXISTING CUSTOMERS
========================================================

Be careful with existing accounts.

If an existing customer already has:

- Company Website
- Valid cached Company Intelligence
- Company Intelligence Ready


DO NOT unnecessarily force them through setup again.


Treat their existing valid website/profile as satisfying the requirement.


If an existing account has no website:

Prompt them to add one before performing NEW AI prospecting that requires
Seller Intelligence.

Do not corrupt existing campaign history or customer data.


========================================================
21. FINAL UX PRINCIPLE
========================================================

The customer experience should feel like hiring and training an AI employee.


The customer should think:

"I gave my employee my company website.
It learned my business.
I can check what it learned.
I can give it extra instructions if I want.
Now it goes to work."


The customer should NOT think:

"I entered a website.
Now I need to manually type my services.
Then I need to refresh something.
Then I need to figure out whether the AI is actually using it."
