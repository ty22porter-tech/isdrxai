====================================================
10. AI EMAIL RELEVANCE QUALITY CHECK
====================================================

Before accepting an AI-generated email, perform an internal relevance check.

Evaluate:

1. Does the email clearly relate to this specific prospect?

2. Does it use a verified signal OR a legitimate business-model connection?

3. Does it connect the prospect's situation to a real seller capability?

4. Does it communicate a business outcome or value?

5. Does it use a relevant differentiator from the Seller Intelligence
Profile?

6. Does it avoid unsupported claims?

7. Does the opening feel relevant enough to make the recipient continue
reading?

8. Does it sound human and professional?

9. Is the CTA easy to respond to?

10. Could this same email be sent unchanged to 100 unrelated companies?


====================================================
11. AUTO-REGENERATE LOW-QUALITY EMAILS
====================================================

If the answer to:

"Could this same email be sent unchanged to 100 unrelated companies?"

is YES,

the email should FAIL the relevance check.

Automatically regenerate the email with stronger personalization.

Also regenerate if:

- No seller capability is connected to the prospect
- No business challenge is identified
- The email is mostly generic service descriptions
- The opening is generic
- The CTA is overly sales-heavy
- Unsupported claims are present


====================================================
12. EMAIL QUALITY SCORE
====================================================

Create an internal Email Relevance Score from 0–100.

Suggested scoring:

+20 = strong prospect-specific opening
+20 = verified or legitimate prospect signal/context
+20 = clear seller capability match
+15 = relevant seller differentiator
+15 = clear business value/outcome
+10 = strong low-friction CTA

Suggested thresholds:

90–100 = Excellent
80–89 = Strong
70–79 = Acceptable
Below 70 = Regenerate

The score does not need to be shown to the user unless useful for debugging
or future admin functionality.

Do not allow weak generic emails to move forward automatically if they score
below the configured threshold.


====================================================
13. EMAIL OPENING RULES
====================================================

Continue enforcing:

Do NOT start with:

"I noticed..."
"I came across..."
"I wanted to reach out..."
"I hope this email finds you well..."
"My name is..."
"We are a leading..."

Start with a business-relevant statement.

The opening should be based on:

- Prospect situation
- Prospect industry
- Verified growth signal
- Operational challenge
- Facility context
- Technology context
- Fleet context
- Hiring context
- Expansion context
- Relevant business outcome


====================================================
14. EMAIL SHOULD ENCOURAGE ENGAGEMENT
====================================================

The objective is not simply to describe the seller.

The reader should feel:

"This is relevant to my company."

The email should encourage a reply or conversation.

Use low-friction CTAs such as:

"Would it be worth comparing how you're handling this today?"

"Is this something your team is evaluating?"

"Would a quick conversation around this make sense?"

"Would it be useful to compare your current approach?"

"How are you handling this today?"

Vary CTA wording.

Do not repeat the same CTA for every prospect.

====================================================

WHAT TO LEAD WITH
