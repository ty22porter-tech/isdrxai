========================================================
1. PURPOSE OF OPPORTUNITY SCORE
========================================================

The Opportunity Score must answer:

"How worthwhile is this company for THIS seller to pursue RIGHT NOW?"

It is NOT:

- a probability of purchase
- an engagement score
- a Google Places quality score
- a generic company score

It is a seller-specific, research-based opportunity assessment.


========================================================

MAX 20

MAX 10

3. COMPANY / ICP FIT — MAX 20
========================================================

Question:

"Is this fundamentally the type of company the seller should target?"

For Fleet & Commercial Vehicle Wraps:

18–20:
Commercial vehicles are central to the business model.

Examples:
- transportation
- logistics
- delivery
- moving
- HVAC
- plumbing
- electrical
- roofing

- restoration
- landscaping
- field-service company

14–17:

8–13:

0–7:

DO NOT award ICP points simply because:

- Google Places found the company
- company has a website
- company has an address
- company has a phone number
