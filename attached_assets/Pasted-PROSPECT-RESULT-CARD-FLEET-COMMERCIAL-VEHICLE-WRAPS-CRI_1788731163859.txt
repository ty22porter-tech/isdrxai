PROSPECT RESULT CARD — FLEET & COMMERCIAL VEHICLE WRAPS

CRITICAL:
When Fleet & Commercial Vehicle Wraps is selected, prospect search results must use the SAME result-card structure and presentation style currently used by IT Services.

Do not simplify the results.

Use the existing IT prospect result card as the reference implementation.

For Fleet & Commercial Vehicle Wraps, each prospect result should include:

1. Fleet Wrap Opportunity Score
2. Why target
3. Potential needs
4. Fleet / branding signal
5. Recommended outreach

Replace IT-specific wording such as:
- IT Opportunity Score
- Compliance
- Cybersecurity needs

with Fleet Wrap-specific intelligence.

EXAMPLE STRUCTURE

Fleet Wrap Opportunity Score: 82 — Strong Fit

Why target:
Explain specifically why this company is a good commercial vehicle wrap prospect based on its industry, business model, fleet usage, growth, locations, hiring activity, branding, or other verified signals.

Potential needs:
List likely relevant services such as:
- Full vehicle wraps
- Partial vehicle wraps
- Fleet graphics
- Vehicle lettering
- New fleet branding
- Replacement vehicle graphics
- Trailer graphics
- Multi-location fleet branding
- Rebranding existing vehicles
- Promotional vehicle graphics

Fleet / Branding Signal:
Summarize the strongest verified prospecting signal.

Examples:
- Operates service vehicles
- Hiring field technicians
- Hiring drivers
- Opening a new location
- Expanding service territory
- Multi-location operation
- Adding vehicles
- Rebranding
- New logo
- Fleet-dependent business model
- Municipal fleet
- University fleet

Recommended outreach:
Provide the best outreach angle for the sales representative based on the prospect's actual business and available buying signals.

The recommendation should explain WHAT the sales rep should lead with.

Examples:
- Offer a fleet branding consultation for newly added service vehicles.
- Lead with consistent branding across multiple locations.
- Discuss wrapping new technician vans as the company expands.
- Position fleet graphics as mobile advertising for a local service business.
- Discuss replacement graphics for municipal vehicles.
- Offer a fleet branding review for campus service and facilities vehicles.

IMPORTANT:
The AI must NOT return generic fleet-wrap explanations for every prospect.

The result must be customized based on:
- Prospect industry
- Company size
- Location
- Decision-maker
- Business model
- Available public research
- Fleet indicators
- Growth signals
- Hiring signals
- Expansion signals
- Branding signals

Do not fabricate fleet size, number of vehicles, purchases, hiring, expansion, rebranding, or other facts.

If a signal cannot be verified, state that it is unconfirmed or base the recommendation on the company's industry/business model only.
