UPDATE iSDRX AI — ADD SIGNAL VERIFICATION + VERIFIED VS INFERRED INTELLIGENCE

IMPORTANT:

Do not redesign Prospect Search or the existing prospect cards.

Extend the current prospect intelligence architecture so that iSDRX AI
clearly separates:

1. VERIFIED FACTS
2. REASONABLE BUSINESS IMPLICATIONS
3. UNVERIFIED / UNKNOWN INFORMATION

The goal is to prevent AI-generated prospect intelligence from presenting
assumptions as facts.


====================================================
1. CORE SIGNAL ACCURACY RULE
====================================================

iSDRX AI must NEVER present an inferred business condition as a verified fact.

Example of incorrect behavior:

"Rescue Air is expanding its fleet."

This statement should NOT be shown unless there is reliable evidence that
the company is actually adding vehicles or expanding its fleet.


Instead, if verified research shows:

"Rescue Air is hiring 12 field technicians"

display:

VERIFIED SIGNAL
Hiring 12 field technicians

POTENTIAL IMPLICATION
Additional field staffing may create future vehicle, fleet branding,
vehicle graphics, or operational needs.

The AI may interpret a verified signal, but it must clearly distinguish
the interpretation from the verified fact.


====================================================
2. SIGNAL CLASSIFICATIONS
====================================================

Create three classifications for prospect intelligence:

VERIFIED

Use when there is direct supporting evidence from a reliable source.

Examples:

- Active job posting
- New location announcement
- Published acquisition
- Company press release
- Public contract award
- Government procurement record
- Published rebranding announcement
- Confirmed new facility
- Confirmed leadership change
- Published fleet purchase
- Publicly announced expansion


POTENTIAL IMPLICATION

Use when the AI is interpreting what a verified signal MAY mean for the
seller.

Examples:

Verified:
Hiring 15 HVAC technicians

Potential implication:
Additional field staffing may increase the need for service vehicles,
vehicle graphics, fleet branding, uniforms, IT support, cleaning, or other
relevant services depending on the seller's capabilities.


UNVERIFIED / UNKNOWN

Use when a fact cannot be confirmed.

Examples:

- Fleet size not confirmed
- Current janitorial provider unknown
- IT provider unknown
- Contract expiration unknown
- Vehicle purchase plans unknown
- Budget unknown
- Square footage unknown
- Expansion not confirmed


====================================================
3. NEVER TURN INDUSTRY FIT INTO A FACT
====================================================

Industry characteristics may be used to determine fit, but they must not be
stated as verified company-specific facts.

Example:

HVAC companies commonly operate service vehicles.

Acceptable:

INDUSTRY FIT
HVAC businesses typically rely on field-service vehicles, making fleet
branding potentially relevant.

Not acceptable:

"This company operates 25 service vans."

unless that number is verified.


====================================================
4. ADD SIGNAL CONFIDENCE
====================================================

Each signal should have a confidence classification.

Use:

HIGH CONFIDENCE
Direct evidence from reliable company, government, job, news, or other
authoritative source.

MEDIUM CONFIDENCE
Strong indirect evidence or multiple corroborating sources.

LOW CONFIDENCE
Industry inference, incomplete evidence, or weak source.

Low-confidence signals must NEVER be written as confirmed facts.

====================================================
6. SOURCE TRACEABILITY
====================================================

For verified signals, retain the supporting source whenever possible.

Examples:

Source:

Source Date:

Source:

====================================================
7. PROSPECT CARD DISPLAY
====================================================

Update prospect cards so intelligence is easier to trust.

Example:

-----------------------------------
VERIFIED SIGNALS
-----------------------------------

✓ Hiring 12 field technicians
Source: Company Careers Page

✓ New Fort Worth location announced
Source: Company Press Release


-----------------------------------
POTENTIAL IMPLICATIONS
-----------------------------------

• Additional field staffing may increase the need for service vehicles.

• Expansion into another territory may create a need for consistent branding
across new vehicles and locations.


-----------------------------------
UNCONFIRMED
-----------------------------------

• Current fleet size

• Planned vehicle purchases

• Current wrap provider


Do not imply that unconfirmed information is known.


====================================================
8. USE VERIFIED SIGNALS IN "WHY TARGET"
====================================================

The Why Target section should prioritize:

VERIFIED SIGNAL
+
SELLER RELEVANCE
+
POTENTIAL BUSINESS IMPACT


Example for Fleet Wraps:

Why Target:

The company is actively hiring additional field technicians, which indicates
growth in its field-service operation.

Because C4 Wraps specializes in commercial vehicle branding and fleet
graphics, additional field staffing may create future opportunities around
branding new service vehicles or maintaining consistency across an expanding
fleet.

Do NOT say:

"The company is expanding its fleet."

unless fleet expansion itself is verified.


====================================================
9. USE VERIFIED SIGNALS IN "POTENTIAL NEEDS"
====================================================

Potential Needs must be written as opportunities, not confirmed purchases.

Good:

Potential Need:
Branding additional service vehicles if field hiring results in fleet growth.

Good:

Potential Need:
Maintaining consistent graphics across vehicles as the operation expands.

Bad:

Potential Need:
Wrap 12 new vans.

The system cannot assume that each new technician receives a new vehicle.


====================================================
10. USE VERIFIED SIGNALS IN EMAIL GENERATION
====================================================

AI-generated emails must follow the same rule.

If the verified signal is:

"Hiring 12 field technicians"

the email may say:

"Adding field technicians can create additional pressure to keep service
vehicles and branding consistent as the operation grows."

Do NOT say:

"I saw you're adding 12 new vans."

unless that is independently verified.


====================================================
11. SIGNAL → IMPLICATION → SELLER VALUE
====================================================

Use this reasoning model:

VERIFIED SIGNAL
        ↓
WHAT COULD THIS SIGNAL MEAN?
        ↓
WHICH BUSINESS CHALLENGE COULD EMERGE?
        ↓
IS THAT CHALLENGE RELEVANT TO THE SELLER?
        ↓
WHICH SELLER CAPABILITY ADDRESSES IT?
        ↓
WHAT BUSINESS VALUE COULD THE SELLER PROVIDE?


Example:

Verified Signal:
Hiring 12 HVAC technicians

Potential Implication:
Field-service capacity is increasing.

Possible Business Challenge:
Maintaining consistent branding as the field operation grows.

Seller Capability:
Commercial fleet graphics and multi-vehicle branding.

Business Value:
Consistent brand presentation and increased local visibility.

Email Angle:
Scaling field operations while maintaining fleet-brand consistency.


====================================================
12. SIGNAL LANGUAGE RULES
====================================================

Use language carefully.

For verified information:

- "The company announced..."
- "The company is hiring..."
- "Public records show..."
- "The company's careers page lists..."
- "The company opened..."
- "The company published..."

For implications:

- "This may indicate..."
- "This could create..."
- "This may increase the need for..."
- "This could make..."
- "This may present an opportunity for..."
- "If this growth results in..."

Avoid:

- "Clearly needs"
- "Definitely needs"
- "Must be expanding"
- "Will need"
- "Is adding vehicles"

unless directly verified.


====================================================
13. MULTIPLE SIGNAL CORROBORATION
====================================================

When multiple signals support the same conclusion, increase confidence but
continue distinguishing facts from conclusions.

Example:

Verified:
Hiring technicians

Verified:
New service territory announced

Verified:
New branch opened

Potential implication:
The company appears to be increasing its field-service capacity.

This is stronger than relying on one signal alone.

However, do not automatically convert:

"increasing field-service capacity"

into:

"buying more vehicles"

without evidence.


====================================================
14. NO-SIGNAL BEHAVIOR
====================================================

If there are no meaningful verified signals, do not invent urgency.

Use:

INDUSTRY FIT
COMPANY BUSINESS MODEL
SELLER VALUE MATCH

Example:

"No recent fleet expansion signal was verified. However, the company operates
in a field-service industry where branded commercial vehicles may play an
important role in local visibility."

This is better than creating a fake trigger event.


====================================================
15. SIGNAL FRESHNESS
====================================================

Where possible, account for signal age.

Suggested categories:

RECENT
0–90 days

ACTIVE
91–180 days

OLDER
181–365 days

STALE
Over 365 days

Prefer recent signals when generating outreach.

Do not use stale signals as if they just happened.


====================================================
16. SIGNAL VALIDATION BEFORE EMAIL SEND
====================================================

Before using a signal in an AI-generated email, verify:

1. Is there a supporting source?

2. Does the source actually support the statement?

3. Is the signal recent enough to still be relevant?

4. Is the email presenting the signal accurately?

5. Is the AI adding an inference?

If yes to #5, the inference must be written as a potential implication,
not a fact.


====================================================
17. EMAIL QUALITY CHECK UPDATE
====================================================

Add this to the existing Email Relevance Quality Check:

SIGNAL ACCURACY CHECK

Before approving an email, ask:

- Does this email contain a prospect-specific factual claim?

If YES:

- Is the claim verified?
- Is there supporting evidence?
- Is it phrased accurately?
- Has an inference accidentally been written as a fact?

If any factual claim cannot be supported, revise the email before approval.


====================================================
18. PROSPECT SCORE SHOULD NOT RELY ON FABRICATED SIGNALS
====================================================

Only verified signals should receive full buying-signal scoring points.

Potential implications may influence the fit score but should receive lower
weight.

Unverified assumptions should receive zero signal points.


Example:

Verified job growth signal:
+20

Verified expansion:
+20

Industry-based potential fleet fit:
+5

Unconfirmed fleet expansion:
+0

====================================================

GOOD:

Verified Signal:

Seller Match:

Recommended Angle:

the current provider is being replaced.

====================================================

"The company has cybersecurity problems."


GOOD:

Verified Signal:
Company is adding multiple locations and hiring additional employees.

Potential Implication:
Additional locations and users may increase endpoint management,
cybersecurity, support, and infrastructure complexity.

Seller Match:
The seller provides managed IT and cybersecurity services.

Recommended Angle:
Discuss how IT support and security are being scaled alongside business
growth.


====================================================
22. CRITICAL ACCURACY PRINCIPLE
====================================================

The system must always distinguish:

WHAT WE KNOW
vs.
WHAT WE THINK IT MAY MEAN

Use this model throughout iSDRX AI:

WHAT WE KNOW
= VERIFIED SIGNAL

WHAT IT MAY MEAN
= POTENTIAL IMPLICATION

WHY IT MATTERS TO THIS SELLER
= VALUE MATCH

HOW TO APPROACH THE PROSPECT
= SALES STRATEGY

WHAT TO SAY
= PERSONALIZED OUTREACH
